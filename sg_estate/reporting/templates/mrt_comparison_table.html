How to read the source layer, centroid association and estate evidence

Row grain and status

A row is one station-code-line membership and carries its reviewed LTA status-source key. Open memberships are reconciled to LTA's 21 July 2026 system map. Non-open rows distinguish under-construction, deferred and planned services; the broader Future filter groups those states for exploration.

Distance and association

Distances use a great-circle calculation between points. The 600 m, 1 km and 1.4 km buckets are geometric diagnostics only. Count columns report estate centroids within 800 m and 1.4 km—not station catchments or households served.

Framework boundary

The nearest estate is a reference anchor. Its Provision score is not a station score; differences inside ±0.3 are not a defensible rank. Liveability bands answer different persona questions and are non-comparable by design.

Value and limitations

HDB and private Value remain separate tenure universes, with direct and proxy bases plus unavailable statuses visible. Below __VALUE_TRUST_THRESHOLD__ transactions, the framework publishes bands rather than decimal precision. Rail context is not a price-growth forecast.

Official sources and derived points

Codes start from the LTA DataMall workbook and are reconciled to the 21 July 2026 system map. Representative points are derived—not official station centroids—from LTA station footprints, LTA exits, or the URA Master Plan rail-station outline for planned sites.

Planned-overlay boundary

The overlay includes TEL/DTL services under construction, physically deferred TEL stations, all announced JRL stages through 2029 and CRL Phase 1 targeted for 2030. Later CRL phases, the Punggol extension and other post-2031 proposals stay in the pipeline model rather than this point overlay.
