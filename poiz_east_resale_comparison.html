Research findings

01

Closest integrated match

Park Place is +6.9% versus Poiz on 2BR PSF, with a +14.8% difference in median 2BR size. Its dual-line PLQ setting is the cleanest East-side test of integration value.

02

Space changes the quantum read

Bedok Residences is -12.6% versus Poiz on 2BR PSF, while its median 2BR is +60.2% different in size. A cheaper PSF can still produce a higher purchase quantum.

03

Liquidity leaders

Parc Esta recorded 112 latest-12-month resales (8.0% of stock); Treasure recorded 135 (6.1%). They are statistical controls, not direct mixed-use matches.

04

Coastal premium is not one thing

Seaside's 18-month all-resale median is S$2,311 PSF. View, floor and facing can dominate project-level averages, so its bedroom tabs must still be size- and unit-position checked.

05

Freehold sensitivity is thin

Katong Regency has only 5 latest-12-month resales (2.0% of stock). It is useful for tenure sensitivity but too thin and structurally different for a pooled Poiz benchmark.

06

Poiz is liquid, not obviously cheap

Poiz's compact units, direct NEL access and daily retail support a repeatable premium. Its healthy exit evidence does not prove underpricing; newer integrated projects and finite lease duration remain the counterweight.
