# Factor Audit — 2026-07-04

Run via the `factor-audit` skill as a multi-agent workflow: three parallel research sweeps
(A international frameworks, B SG open data, C buyer priorities), merged + axiom-pre-screened,
then **per-candidate validation by two independent agents** — a buyer-importance validator
(survey/revealed-preference evidence that real buyers weigh the factor when choosing an estate)
and an adversarial skeptic (instructed to reject via Axioms A1–A3 / Guardrails G1–G4, defaulting
to rejection when uncertain). A candidate is proposed only if buyer importance ≥ MEDIUM **and**
the skeptic could not find an axiom violation **and** the G1 discrimination probe clears the
±0.3 noise floor.

All three sweeps ran with **live web access** (unlike the 2026-06-19 desk-synthesis audit).
Dataset IDs were checked live on 2026-07-04 unless marked `[verify]`.

Baseline: the framework's current **20 components** (v2.0). Everything adopted from the
2026-06-19 audit (air_quality, air_noise, eldercare, stewardship, jtc_industrial, hawker_v2,
hdb_density, BCA construction-disruption via D) was excluded from candidacy.

Headline: **8 candidates survived the merge pre-screen; 2 survived validation.**

## 1. Critical gaps (propose adding)

### `hosp_acute` — Acute hospital / A&E proximity
- **One-line description**: Distance-decay to nearest 24h A&E public acute hospital, with
  community hospitals as a secondary tier. The existing `hlth` component scores only CHAS
  clinics + polyclinics; the acute tier is currently unscored.
- **Provenance estimate**: MEASURED (fixed facility list, ~10 acute hospitals, hand-verifiable).
- **Proposed weight bucket**: implement as a **sub-metric inside `hlth`** (currently 0.04),
  not a new weight line — `is_upgrade_not_new`. Suggested internal split: clinics/polyclinics
  0.6 / acute tier 0.4 of `hlth`. No change to the 20-component table needed.
- **SG dataset(s)**: MOH health-facilities collection (data.gov.sg collection 521) + hand-curated
  A&E flag (~10 rows); geocode via OneMap (same flow as `fetch_chas.py`). Update cadence: hospital
  openings are rare, gazetted years ahead (next: Eastern General ~2030) — annual refresh suffices.
- **Discriminating power**: verified spread — JURONG EAST 0.6 km to NTFGH, SENGKANG 0.8 km to SKH,
  YISHUN 1.1 km to KTPH vs CHOA CHU KANG 6.1 km, MARINE PARADE 5.8 km, BUKIT PANJANG 5.7 km
  (≈5.0 vs ≈2.2–2.4 on a distance-decay score). Mid-field estates (BUKIT MERAH, BUKIT BATOK,
  HOUGANG, CLEMENTI) cluster indistinguishably at ~2.5–2.6 km — the tails carry the signal.
- **Buyer validation (MEDIUM, survey-grade)**: ERA My Dream Home Survey 2025 — 27% of buyers
  prioritise hospital proximity (8th of 10 amenities). Portals (99.co, EdgeProp) maintain
  dedicated "near hospital" search filters. Counter-evidence honestly noted: Stacked Homes' 66-condo
  TTSH distance-band analysis found **no price premium**, and immediate adjacency is often
  viewed negatively (sirens, taboo) — the construct is *access within the estate*, not adjacency,
  and the distance-decay function should plateau rather than reward 0 m.
- **Skeptic verdict**: pass — "tried hard to reject; could not." A1: place fact. A2: SG
  deliberately sites regional hospitals in heartlands (Yishun, Sengkang, Jurong East, Woodlands);
  the far estates (CCK, AMK, Bukit Panjang, Marine Parade) show no income gradient.
- **Cross-references**: found by all 3 sweeps; borders `hlth`.

### `mixed_use` — Master Plan mixed-use land share
- **One-line description**: Fraction of commercial / white / business-park zoning within the
  estate buffer, from URA Master Plan land-use polygons.
- **Provenance estimate**: MEASURED (gazetted polygons; a zoning fraction is not disputable).
- **Proposed weight bucket**: 0.02–0.03 as a new component (skeptic's G1 arithmetic: needs
  ≥0.03 with near-full-range variation to clear the floor decisively; at 0.02 it clears only on
  the extreme tails). Weight source: shave `dens` or `amen`. **Weight decision is the user's** —
  not applied in this audit.
- **SG dataset(s)**: URA Master Plan 2019 Land Use (`d_90d86daa5bfaa371668b84fa5f01424f`) +
  Symbol Text/GPR layer (`d_dc4918b82cb79cbb706c8f35bc412242`). Update cadence: Master Plan
  revisions ~5-yearly with rolling amendments; re-ingest on each gazetted revision (MP2025 due —
  re-check the dataset ID then).
- **Discriminating power**: verified spread — JURONG EAST (Jurong Lake District), PAYA
  LEBAR/GEYLANG, PUNGGOL (Digital District), WOODLANDS and TAMPINES (Regional Centres),
  QUEENSTOWN (one-north fringe) vs near-zero CHOA CHU KANG, SEMBAWANG, PASIR RIS, BUKIT PANJANG,
  YISHUN, SENGKANG.
- **Buyer validation (MEDIUM, survey-grade)**: ERA 2025 — 57% rank proximity to
  workplace/commercial vibrancy as top satisfaction driver; Knight Frank (via EdgeProp) reports a
  quantified 20–25% launch premium for integrated mixed-use developments (project-level, honestly
  noted as imperfect mapping to estate-level zoning share); PropertyGuru/PropertyLimBrothers
  publish guides teaching buyers to read Master Plan zoning — expert-mediated use of this exact
  dataset.
- **Skeptic verdict**: pass. A2 breaks decisively: the high-scoring estates (Jurong East,
  Woodlands, Punggol) are heartland towns, not prestige districts.
- **Cross-references**: Sweep B only, but with a named live dataset and clean axioms; borders
  `dens`.

## 3. Nice-to-have (lower priority)

All six failed validation but pass at least the axioms in part; each has a named revisit trigger.

- **Residential carpark provision (lots-per-dwelling)** — buyer MEDIUM (Tengah/Bidadari car-lite
  anxiety is real; HDB/LTA issued official reassurance), discrimination real (TENGAH/WOODLEIGH vs
  TAMPINES/PASIR RIS). **Rejected A3**: live check (2026-07-04) shows the static HDB Carpark
  Information dataset has **no lot-count or lot-type field** (only carpark number, address, x/y,
  type) — lots-per-dwelling cannot honestly be tagged MEASURED from it. Revisit if HDB publishes
  lot counts, or re-scope to the carpark-availability API's `total_lots` (real-time, needs a
  capture pipeline; provenance PARTLY_MEASURED).
- **Active-mobility & walkability network** — buyer MEDIUM (PropertyGuru CSS; HDB Sample
  Household Survey 2023/24 "ease of cycling" satisfaction fell 90.9%→86.6%). **Rejected G1
  overlap**: PCN continuity is already a `green` sub-metric, park connectors feed `sport`,
  first/last-mile shelter feeds `infra`/covered-linkway; the novel residual (intersection density)
  barely separates standardized HDB precinct fabric, and the ICN rollout to all towns by 2030
  compresses the cycling-km spread. Wiring the existing `ingest_pedestrian_paths.py` stub remains
  a worthwhile *engineering* task for the sub-metrics already owed to `conn`/`green`.
- **Gazetted heritage / built character** — the strongest rejected candidate on evidence quality:
  Ti & Tan (2020, Land Use Policy 91, ~1M transactions) find a genuine conservation-designation
  price spillover. **Rejected G1 arithmetic**: at the self-capped 0.01–0.02 weight, a full-range
  swing moves scores at most 0.1–0.2 (< ±0.3), and ~27 of 35 estates score near-zero (Tiong
  Bahru/Katong/Chinatown carry all variance). Revisit only if the framework ever re-bands weights.
- **Coffeeshop / neighbourhood F&B diversity** — buyer MEDIUM (PropertyGuru CSS H1-2022
  millennials; mature-vs-non-mature threads). **Rejected G1 saturation**: HDB's planned
  ~1-coffeeshop-per-neighbourhood-centre norm compresses mature towns to near-uniform, and the
  candidate barely separates its own motivating pair (Sengkang vs Punggol). SFA address-level
  export also unconfirmed `[verify]`.
- **After-death & heavy-utility disamenity** — buyer MEDIUM (2015 Fernvale Lea: 95 BTO buyers
  demanded refunds over a columbarium announcement — hard behavioural evidence). **Rejected G1
  hyper-locality**: price impact detectable only within ~250 m (SRX); Dr Wealth found no
  significant estate-level difference controlling for MRT/amenities; the heavy-utility half is
  ~redundant with `jtc_industrial`. Block-level, not estate-level; out of scope at this unit of
  analysis.
- **Persistent dengue / vector burden** — epidemiologically real place attribute (Seidahmed 2018;
  drainage/built-form persistence), but buyer importance LOW (appears in **no** buyer survey;
  portal content is advisory, not choice-driving; Yishun's dengue recurrence coexists with rising
  resale prices) and G1 fails on cluster transience at estate grain. NEA cluster GeoJSON remains
  a candidate *Liveability alert layer* rather than a Provision component.

## 4. Principled rejections

Documented so the next audit does not re-litigate. Grouped by violated rule.

**A2 (class/income laundering):**
- Crime environment / night-safety by NPC (Sweeps A+C; B rejected it independently) — residual
  variance presumed income-correlated; SG physical-crime variance also sub-floor (G1).
- Social-mix / SEIFA-style deprivation index (Sweep A) — income composition by construction.
- School quality rankings / cut-off proxies (Sweep C) — MOE bans rankings; proxies launder income.
- "Atas area" / address prestige / D9-D10 cachet (Sweep C) — the canonical trap, phrased innocently.
- "Good class of neighbours" / no rental blocks / no worker dorms (Sweep C) — explicitly banned.

**A1 (person-relative, not place):**
- Job accessibility / commute-time index (Sweep A) — also already handled by `employment_model.py`.
- Commute / "near my office" / "near parents" / distance-to-CBD (Sweep C).
- "Within 1 km of MY chosen school" (Sweep C) — school *supply* is already `sch`.

**A3 (dishonest provenance):**
- Perceptual streetscape quality — Place Pulse / street-imagery ML (Sweep A) — JUDGED dressed as
  MEASURED, plus Place Pulse's "wealthy/safe" axis is an A2 trap.
- Perceived-safety surveys (Sweep A) — survey averages are JUDGED.
- BCA/SG Enable "Friendly Buildings" barrier-free dataset (Sweep B) — ~520 self-assessed buildings
  islandwide; too sparse to score 23+ towns honestly. Revisit if the Accessibility Code compliance
  register opens.

**G1 (saturated / sub-noise-floor in SG):**
- Digital infrastructure (fibre/5G); water/utility resilience; power-outage reliability (SAIDI ~9
  s/customer/yr); mobile coverage; SCDF emergency response (deliberately equalized);
  consumer-goods availability; public library proximity (one per town by design — Sweeps B+C
  proposed, Sweep A's own note killed it); SPF crime stats by NPC (dataset real —
  `d_5767147db6e5b4c4cfa874db132fef39` — construct fails, not the data).

**G2 (forward-looking positives route through momentum):**
- Zoning-stability / rezoning-momentum bonus from Master Plan amendment layers — the amendment
  layers remain useful as a *momentum input* to `pipeline_data.json`, never a Provision bonus.

**G3 (Value-side):** housing affordability / rent burden (Mercer factor).

**G4 (unit-level):** unit design quality, block orientation, view corridors.

**Duplicates / already covered (no axiom needed):** 15-minute-city composite (re-weighting of
existing components); kindergartens (ECDA listing already covers preschool — at most refresh the
childcare ingester to the consolidated listing); OneMap sports_facilities theme (already `sport`);
PUB drainage/ABC Waters (flood already MEASURED; ABC sites too few — parked); "quiet estate / not
crowded" (dens + noise + air_noise); "walkable in rain" (covered linkways in `infra`); "lift
always break down / upkeep" (stewardship, adopted 2026-06-19).

## 5. Liveability-side candidates (NOT Provision)

- **Places of worship / religious-amenity proximity** — fails the A1 boundary test in its only
  meaningful form (which institution counts depends on the resident's faith; an aggregate
  "any-worship-place" count measures nothing anyone experiences). Route as a persona-conditional
  amenity delta if ever wanted; most relevant to Retiree/YoungFam personas.
- **Dengue cluster proximity as a T0 alert** — plausible Liveability-matrix annotation
  (transient, person-relevant risk tolerance) rather than a Provision score input.
- Value-side, noted and routed: affordability/rent burden → `value_model.py`.
- Unit-level, noted and routed: orientation/floor/view → out of scope.
