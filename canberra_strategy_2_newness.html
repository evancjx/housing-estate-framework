How to use the newness controls

Different controls answer different buyer questions; combining them into one score would erase the explanation.

2024 lease

Canberra Crescent

Developer New Sale positioning and the youngest lease clock. Launch-month medians show the booked/released unit mix, not appreciation.

2020 lease

Watergardens

Close newness and geography control. Sub Sales test a near-completion market state but are structurally different from developer sales.

2020 lease

The Commodore

Similar lease vintage with actual Resale evidence, useful for studying an emerging exit market while retaining Canberra location.

2010 lease

Canberra Residences

Older lease/layout control. A PSF discount may purchase more area; remaining lease and condition need separate diligence.

Not appreciation

No exact apartment identifiers are available, so these pages make no repeat-sale claim. Period-to-period medians are compositional evidence and must not be used as unit growth rates.

Sources and calculation register

Lease start is parsed from URA tenure text. Elapsed lease years are a simple as-of-year diagnostic and are not a legal remaining-lease calculation.

  • URA Property Market InformationOfficial transaction price, month, area, floor band, tenure and sale state. URA notes that resale/subsale rows are caveats and caveat lodgement is not mandatory.
  • URA transaction API referenceOfficial field definitions and five-year API coverage.
  • OneMapAuthoritative national geospatial reference used by the project location pipeline.
  • LTA rail networkOperational rail context. Distances on these pages are computed straight-line, not routes.
  • Watergardens developer brochureDeveloper disclosure of land tenure and project particulars; transaction evidence remains URA PMI.
