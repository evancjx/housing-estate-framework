The captured official 2026Q2 rental series does not establish a current subject rental median for this comparison. Rental fallback is unproven here; a missing statistic is neither zero rent nor a licence to substitute a neighbour's rent.

- There are only eight recent New Sales; the most-observed >100–130 sqm cohort has three sales at a S$3.273m median. That is too thin to price every residual layout.
- The official return shows no reported sales in June–August despite nine homes remaining in August. This warrants checking remaining formats and quotations, not assuming discounts.
