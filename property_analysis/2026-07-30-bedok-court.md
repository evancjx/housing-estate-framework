# Bedok Court — resale inventory and quantum analysis

## Decision

Bedok Court is a **deep-value space and future-Bedok-South-node proposition**,
but its approximately 55–56 remaining lease years make the exit case severely
lease-constrained. The unusually large layouts can be excellent for own stay.
They do not create automatic investment arbitrage: lower PSF compensates for
age, capital works, financing/CPF sensitivities and a narrower future buyer
pool.

The currently advertised approximately 1,195 sqft unit around **S$1.45m** is
not compelling from public evidence alone. Three recent approximately
1,173 sqft resales achieved S$1.25m, S$1.265m and S$1.27m. A renovated,
high-floor or better-facing 1,195 sqft unit can deserve a premium, but the
seller must prove roughly S$180k–S$200k of difference.

The conditional entry ladder is:

1. **At or below S$1.30m:** evidence-aligned for approximately 1,173–1,195
   sqft, subject to lease, condition and financing checks.
2. **S$1.31m–S$1.38m:** defensible only for materially better condition,
   floor, view or vacant possession.
3. **S$1.39m–S$1.45m:** premium territory; obtain direct same-layout valuation
   support.
4. **Above S$1.45m:** unattractive for an ordinary small-format Bedok Court
   unit.

Larger 1,733 sqft and 2,271–2,411 sqft units form separate markets. Their
recent achieved ranges are roughly S$1.70m–S$1.80m and S$2.33m–S$2.46m
respectively. Current larger-unit asks around S$2.56m–S$2.69m are premium
unless condition and layout justify them.

At the conditional S$1.30m basis, eight-year 3% annual price growth gives about
**S$264k capital-only profit** before financing and holding costs. A S$1m
capital-only profit requires an exit near **S$2.402m**, nearly double entry.
That is not a credible base case for the smallest ordinary segment as its
remaining lease approaches the high-40s. Bedok Court has **own-stay utility
potential, but not huge risk-adjusted investment quantum**.

## Scope and evidence rules

This is a property analysis, not a factor-audit report or unified ranking. It
separates:

- **Advertised inventory:** asks and portal cards, not achieved prices or proof
  of continued availability.
- **Achieved evidence:** URA-derived resale caveats and rental contracts, which
  do not fully expose condition, facing, incentives or defects.
- **Scenario underwriting:** explicit sensitivities, not a forecast.

The primary achieved-price reference is
[URA Property Market Information](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/property-market-information/pmiResidentialTransactionSearch).
Caveats are voluntarily lodged and not exhaustive. Exact strata area, floor
plan, block, floor and stack matter more than unreliable portal bedroom tags.

## Project proposition

The
[99.co Bedok Court project record](https://www.99.co/singapore/condos-apartments/bedok-court)
describes a 280-unit condominium completed around 1985, with three residential
addresses and facilities including pool, tennis, squash, clubhouse, parking,
playground and security. The captured URA data states a 99-year lease
commencing in 1982, leaving about **55–56 years**.

That remaining lease is the central underwriting issue. A five-year exit would
offer roughly 50–51 years; an eight-year exit roughly 47–48. Loan tenure, CPF
use and buyer eligibility are person- and rule-specific. Obtain advice for the
actual buyer and model the future buyer rather than declaring the property
generically financeable or unfinanceable.

The positive side is rare internal space at manageable total price. The
negative side is that large, older homes can need expensive electrical,
plumbing, air-conditioning, window and waterproofing work in addition to MCST
capital projects.

## Collective-sale treatment

Bedok Court's age can attract en-bloc speculation, but no current collective
sale tender or required consent threshold was verified in the sweep.
[Strata Titles Boards guidance](https://www.stratatb.gov.sg/how-do-i-apply/matters-under-ltsa/)
generally requires 80% by share value and strata area for developments at least
ten years old, followed by the statutory process. Owner consensus, reserve
price, planning, development charges, lease top-up and developer appetite are
all uncertain. The base case assigns **zero en-bloc value**.

## Underwriting and future quantum

The representative scenario uses the conditional target for a
1,173–1,195 sqft unit: **S$1.30m**, not the S$1.45m public ask. BSD is
**S$36,600** under current
[IRAS residential BSD tiers](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29).
The illustration assumes dutiable value equals entry price; actual BSD and
ABSD apply to the higher of purchase consideration or market value.
The no-ABSD case assumes a Singapore citizen buying a first residential
property.

`capital-only profit = exit price × 0.975 − entry price − BSD − S$5,000 acquisition legal/technical allowance`

The assumed 2.5% selling friction and fixed S$5,000 acquisition allowance are
included. Scenarios exclude any other legal fees, financing, maintenance,
renovation, property tax, rent, vacancy and opportunity cost. The
second-property sensitivity deducts **20% of entry**, the current
[SC second-property ABSD rate](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29).

| Hold / annual price growth | Exit quantum | SC first-property capital-only profit | SC second-property after 20% ABSD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 years / 0% | S$1.300m | -S$74k | -S$334k |
| 5 years / 2% | S$1.435m | S$58k | -S$202k |
| 5 years / 3% | S$1.507m | S$128k | -S$132k |
| 5 years / 4% | S$1.582m | S$201k | -S$59k |
| 8 years / 0% | S$1.300m | -S$74k | -S$334k |
| 8 years / 2% | S$1.523m | S$143k | -S$117k |
| 8 years / 3% | S$1.647m | S$264k | S$4k |
| 8 years / 4% | S$1.779m | S$393k | S$133k |

Capital-only break-even is about 1.1% annually over five years or 0.7% over
eight before holding costs. That apparently low hurdle does not include major
renovation or an age-related resale discount. Current
[SSD rules](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property)
apply for four years to purchases from 4 July 2025; modelled exits avoid SSD
only if dates and rules remain unchanged.

## Catalysts

- Very large internal spaces at low entry PSF and manageable small-format
  quantum.
- TEL5 and Bedok South integrated-node completion can improve access and daily
  amenities.
- Scarcity of comparable legacy floor plates supports own-stay demand.
- A purchase close to the latest S$1.25m–S$1.27m caveats preserves headroom.

## Risks

- Approximately 55–56 lease years remain now and 47–48 after eight years.
- Financing, CPF and future buyer-pool sensitivity can outweigh nearby
  infrastructure uplift.
- Ageing lifts, pipes, electrical systems, façades, roofs, waterproofing, pool,
  courts and carparks can require large expenditure or special levies.
- Thin turnover and large total quantum reduce exit liquidity.
- Special-layout PSF can mislead because patios, stairs or voids inflate strata
  area.
- En-bloc is unverified optionality, not an investment thesis.

## Due diligence before an offer

1. Before paying an option fee, obtain title, exact lease dates, strata plan,
   floor plan, share value and measured usable internal area.
2. Review MCST accounts and AGM minutes, sinking fund, reserve study, insurance
   claims, major works, special levies and collective-sale documentation.
3. Inspect plumbing, electrical load, windows, water ingress, balcony or patio
   membranes, lifts, roof, façade, pool, courts and carpark.
4. Confirm tenancy, vacant possession and renovation approvals.
5. Obtain buyer-specific mortgage and CPF advice plus a bank valuation that
   recognises the remaining lease.

## Final assessment

Bedok Court can make sense as a **space-led own-stay purchase near
S$1.25m–S$1.30m for the approximately 1,173–1,195 sqft segment**. It should not
be bought at S$1.45m merely because its PSF looks low beside a new project. The
future Bedok South node is useful, but disciplined entry, a healthy MCST and a
buyer-specific lease/finance plan are more important than speculative huge
quantum.
