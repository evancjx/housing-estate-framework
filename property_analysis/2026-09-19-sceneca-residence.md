## Decision

Sceneca Residence subsales now clear above every matching developer price,
so the July discount thesis no longer holds and the project looks fairly
priced rather than cheap. This capture supersedes the 30 July 2026 analysis.
The proposition is unchanged: direct Tanah Merah MRT access, retail below, a
young 99-year lease and only 268 homes should keep long-run owner-occupier
and tenant relevance. What has changed is the price evidence.

A fresh URA District 16 export shows **ten secondary-market Sub Sales**, not
the two available in July. Eight are new to this analysis, including five in
August 2026 alone. Every one of them cleared **above the highest developer
price for the same exact size**, by 1.7% to 11.0%, and about 8.5% to 16.7%
above the matching developer median. Recent subsales cluster tightly at
**S$2,279–S$2,398 psf**, almost regardless of unit size, and eight of the ten
are on floors 06–10.

That changes two conclusions from July:

1. **The handover seller wave is being absorbed.** July read the dozens of
   portal advertisements as a supply overhang without proven liquidity.
   Sellers are in fact exiting, and buyers are paying above launch prices to
   take their units.
2. **Several July buy boxes are now below the achieved market.** The 753,
   904 and 1,044 sqft ranges sat under prices that have since changed hands.
   Treat those old ranges as bargain thresholds, not as realistic targets.

The July lead — a 753 sqft advertisement at S$1.50m — would now sit about
13% below two achieved 753 sqft subsales (S$1.73m and S$1.75m). Assume it is
gone, stale or not what it appeared to be unless a seller confirms it in
writing.

Revised evidence-backed buy boxes, for conventional mid-floor stacks:

1. **689 sqft two-bedroom:** S$1.57m–S$1.60m, directly supported by two
   August subsales. The best-evidenced entry point.
2. **753 sqft:** S$1.70m–S$1.75m. S$1.80m needs a higher floor or better
   stack to justify it.
3. **904 sqft:** S$2.05m–S$2.10m for floors 06–10; up to about S$2.17m for
   floors 11–15.
4. **1,044 sqft:** about S$2.45m. The July S$2.50m ask is now only about 2%
   above the one achieved subsale.
5. **1,163 sqft:** about S$2.69m. The low end of July's S$2.68m–S$2.79m asks
   is now at market, not "highly selective".

Higher prices also compress the rental fallback to about **2.9%–3.1% gross at
URA's 2026Q2 median rent** (2.5%–3.4% across its quartile band), and the
illustrated capital-only break-even rises with the entry price.
Sceneca now looks like a fairly priced, liquid-enough completed project, not
an obvious discount.

## Scope and evidence rules

This is a property analysis, not a factor-audit report and not a unified
property ranking. It separates:

- **advertised inventory**, which proves only that a card was published;
- **achieved transactions and rents**, which are stronger but do not disclose
  every unit attribute, incentive or tenancy term; and
- **scenario underwriting**, which is transparent arithmetic rather than a
  forecast.

The primary achieved-price source is
[URA Property Market Information](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/property-market-information/pmiResidentialTransactionSearch).
For this capture a fresh D16 apartment/condominium export covering January
2025 to September 2026 was downloaded at the timestamp above; the latest
recorded sale month in that export is September 2026. Earlier developer sales
come from the repository's committed URA-derived ledger. The export was split
by **Type of Sale** before analysis. Developer new sales are not described as
resale liquidity. Caveats are voluntary and lodged with a lag, so recent
months are likely incomplete; URA explains its broader data methodology in
[REALIS coverage and methodology](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/reis/coverageandMethodology).

Sale dates refer to displayed contract/caveat months, not necessarily the day
an option was granted.

**Advertised prices were not re-captured.** Public portals refused automated
retrieval at this capture. All asking-price evidence below is carried forward
from 30 July 2026 and is labelled as such. Treat it as seven weeks stale.

## Project proposition and completion-status check

[MCC Land's current project page](https://property.mcc.sg/) describes Sceneca
as a 99-year, 268-unit mixed-use project at Tanah Merah with a 2026 TOP
indication. URA awarded the 8,880 sqm site for S$248.99m, with a maximum
24,864 sqm gross floor area and commercial use on the first storey:
[URA tender award](https://www.ura.gov.sg/Corporate/Media-Room/Media-Releases/pr20-33).
That works out to roughly **S$930 psf per plot ratio** by calculation.

The residential towers have 268 homes across 26 and 28 Tanah Merah Kechil
Link. The public project record describes two blocks, up to 15 storeys and a
direct connection to Tanah Merah MRT. URA-derived transaction records report a
99-year lease commencing in 2021, leaving roughly **93–94 years** at the
research date.

Public completion signals conflict:

- MCC's current page indicates TOP in 2026;
- an older
  [appointed-agency fact sheet](https://img.huttons.sg/upload/broke/d379d488126c4abf9505367876588b57/1507deee20b245dab7f4f174a638c905/pdf/20221103/Seceneca%20Fact%20Sheet%20Htns.pdf)
  states expected vacant possession by 31 May 2027; and
- the 99.co project ledger displayed approximately 20 rental contracts in May
  and June 2026, indicating that leasing or occupation activity is already
  appearing in the public data.

Every captured secondary transaction is still recorded as a **Sub Sale**, not
a Resale. That is consistent with sellers transferring before the project's
Certificate of Statutory Completion. Do not infer a unit's legal handover state
from any one portal. Before purchase, sight the project's actual TOP/CSC
documentation, the unit's key-handover record, defects status and whether
maintenance contributions have commenced.

The same fact sheet indicated approximately 1,000 sqm each of supermarket and
other retail/F&B space, 214 ordinary car lots plus accessible lots, and 68
bicycle lots. The retail podium improves daily convenience but is not a
full-scale institutional mall. Tenant mix, loading/service activity and
strata-retail operations remain items to inspect.

## Location, demand and future optionality

Tanah Merah MRT provides East–West Line access and the branch to Changi
Airport. Direct connection reduces walking-weather friction and supports both
commuter and airport-linked tenants. Sceneca Square adds a supermarket and
daily retail; Bedok Mall, Changi Business Park, the airport and East Coast
amenities broaden the catchment.

The most important future transport option is real but long-dated. LTA plans
to convert the existing Tanah Merah–Changi Airport branch into part of the
Thomson–East Coast Line and extend it to Terminal 5, creating direct airport-
to-city services in the next decade:
[LTA TEL/CRL airport announcement](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2025/7/news-releases/TELe_and_CRL_changi_airport_to_city_centre.html).
This is a planned network benefit, not today's journey pattern and not a
guaranteed unit-level price uplift.

LTA is also exploring community uses beneath the Tanah Merah MRT viaduct:
[Tanah Merah viaduct plans](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/getting_around/public_transport/a_better_public_transport_experience/transforming-tanah-merah-mrt-viaduct-into-community-spaces.html).
Treat this as liveability optionality until delivery, not bankable value.

Public project pages name several nearby schools, but Primary 1 distance
priority is exact-address and annual-rule specific. Verify the block and school
in [MOE SchoolFinder](https://www.moe.gov.sg/schoolfinder) instead of relying
on a sales claim.

The same location also creates competition. Optima @ Tanah Merah, Urban Vista,
Casa Merah and other D16 projects give buyers and tenants substitutes.
Sceneca's young lease and integration justify a premium, but do not remove
price sensitivity.

## Principal risks

- **Secondary evidence is still thin:** ten subsales, eight of them mid-floor
  and five in one month, cannot establish a growth rate or a floor premium.
- **No true resales yet:** every secondary row is a pre-completion Sub Sale.
  Post-completion resale pricing remains untested.
- **The premium may be a handover effect:** completion, SSD expiry and fresh
  handover condition can lift early subsales. The first post-completion
  resales may not hold an 11% premium over developer pricing.
- **Status/documentation conflict:** public TOP/VP signals differ; inspect the
  actual statutory and unit handover documents.
- **Integration premium is already capitalised:** buyers paid for MRT and
  retail at launch, and subsale buyers have now paid again on top.
- **New-project operating risk:** defects, MCST budgets, sinking fund,
  maintenance standards and retail operations have little history.
- **Mixed-use and rail externalities:** track/platform announcements, road
  traffic, retail loading, food odour, privacy and pedestrian activity differ
  by stack.
- **Car parking is not one-for-one with homes:** households needing multiple
  cars should examine by-laws and availability.
- **Substitute supply:** nearby Tanah Merah and wider D16 projects limit
  project-wide pricing power.
- **Catalyst timing:** airport-line conversion and community-space plans are
  long-dated or uncompleted.
- **Policy and carrying costs:** ABSD can erase much of the investment return,
  while current SSD penalises a sale within four years for recent purchases.
- **Stale ask and rent evidence:** this capture refreshed achieved prices
  only; portal asks and rents are from 30 July.

## Due diligence before an option to purchase

1. Re-capture current asks and confirm the exact unit, seller identity and
   written executable price. Discard any card priced far below the subsale
   band unless the seller can explain the discount.
2. Obtain a bank valuation and compare it with the exact-size subsale rows
   above, matching the floor band. Do not price a high floor off mid-floor
   evidence, or the reverse.
3. Sight TOP, CSC where applicable, key-handover and defect rectification
   documents for the project and unit.
4. Inspect peak and off-peak MRT/road noise, west sun, privacy, retail loading,
   smells, pedestrian paths and future obstruction.
5. Review the strata plan for efficient internal area and all balcony,
   planter, void or private-enclosed-space components.
6. Review the sale and purchase agreement, title/caveat, encumbrances, seller's
   purchase date and any seller SSD exposure.
7. Obtain MCST maintenance and sinking-fund budgets, by-laws, AGM material,
   insurance, carpark rights and known defects or special levies.
8. If tenanted, verify the signed tenancy, deposit, rent receipts, inventory,
   diplomatic/break clauses and vacant-possession obligation.
9. Model buyer-specific ABSD, loan-to-value, TDSR, CPF/cash needs and an
   interest-rate and vacancy stress case.

## Final assessment

Sceneca Residence keeps **high liveability relevance**. On capital upside,
the evidence has moved from "discount-dependent" to **"fairly priced with
demonstrated early liquidity"**. Ten subsales above every matching developer
high show real secondary demand. They also mean the easy margin has gone:
buyers now pay about S$2,280–S$2,400 psf, not launch PSF.

At an achieved S$1.73m for 753 sqft, a first-property buyer needs about 6%
gross growth just to break even after costs, the gross rental fallback is
about 3.1% at URA's median rent, and a S$500,000 capital-only profit needs
about S$3,121 psf on exit. The rational strategy is unchanged in form but not
in numbers: buy a verified unit at or slightly below the exact-size subsale
evidence for its floor band, and do not pay for a further rerating before any
true post-completion resale confirms it. Do not choose a format for yield:
URA's project median implies about 2.9%–3.1% across every size, so the
entry price and the unit's position in the S$5.07–S$6.50 psf rent band decide
the outcome.

This report is a point-in-time research snapshot, not valuation, legal, tax or
financial advice. Advertisements, caveats, rents, project status and tax rules
can change after the capture timestamp and must be refreshed before an offer.
