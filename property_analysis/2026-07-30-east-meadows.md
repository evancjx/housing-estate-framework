Research captured: **2026-07-30 22:48:38 SGT (UTC+08:00)**

## Decision

East Meadows is a **conditional buy at a caveat-led price**, not a
high-confidence “huge quantum” opportunity. Its 482-unit scale, practical
family layouts and short advertised walk to Tanah Merah MRT support resale and
rentability. The constraints are a lease that began in 1998, an ageing
facility envelope and live three-bedroom asks that often sit above the latest
matching achieved prices.

For a normal approximately 1,195 sqft three-bedroom unit, the best current
public lead is around **S$1.518m**. Recent same-size resales span S$1.43m on a
low floor in July 2026, S$1.605m in June and S$1.54m in May. That makes
S$1.50m–S$1.56m a defensible negotiation zone after adjusting for floor,
facing, renovation and tenancy. It does not make every S$1.7m advertisement
fair.

The conditional entry ladder is:

1. **At or below S$1.50m:** attractive for a standard 1,195–1,249 sqft unit if
   condition and stack checks are clean.
2. **S$1.50m–S$1.58m:** evidence-aligned fair value; insist on a unit-specific
   bank valuation and recent exact-size caveats.
3. **S$1.59m–S$1.64m:** acceptable only for a materially better floor, view,
   renovation or vacant-possession profile.
4. **S$1.65m and above:** premium territory for an ordinary three-bedroom
   layout; the seller needs to prove the unit-specific advantage.

At the S$1.52m underwriting basis, an eight-year 3% annual price-growth
scenario yields about **S$307k capital-only profit**, before financing and all
holding costs. A S$1m capital-only profit would require an exit near
**S$2.637m**. That is a bull outcome, not a decision-grade base case. The
project therefore has **moderate absolute-dollar potential, but not huge
risk-adjusted quantum potential** at current fair value.

## Scope and evidence rules

This is a property analysis, not a factor-audit report or unified property
ranking. It separates:

- **Advertised inventory:** seller asks and portal cards, which may be stale,
  duplicated or withdrawn.
- **Achieved evidence:** URA-derived resale caveats and rental contracts,
  which are stronger but do not expose full renovation, facing or incentive
  details.
- **Scenario underwriting:** transparent assumptions, not a valuation or
  forecast.

The achieved-price reference is
[URA Property Market Information](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/property-market-information/pmiResidentialTransactionSearch).
Resale caveats are voluntarily lodged and are not exhaustive. Bedroom tags on
portals are secondary to exact strata area, floor plan, block, floor and stack.

## Project proposition

The
[99.co East Meadows project record](https://www.99.co/singapore/condos-apartments/east-meadows)
describes a 482-unit condominium completed around 2001. The captured URA
records state a 99-year lease commencing in 1998, leaving approximately
**70–71 years** at the research date. That is still serviceable for an
ordinary holding period, but an eight-year buyer should underwrite the next
purchaser seeing only roughly 62–63 years.

The core proposition is straightforward: larger family floor plates at a much
lower PSF than newer Tanah Merah stock, with a short advertised walk to the
East-West Line. Lower PSF is not automatically a discount because age,
remaining lease, maintenance liability and less efficient legacy layouts are
part of the price.

## Rental fallback

The
[99.co project rental ledger](https://www.99.co/singapore/condos-apartments/east-meadows)
shows recent 1,100–1,400 sqft three-bedroom contracts mostly around
**S$4,100–S$4,700 per month**, with occasional higher observations. A live
[1,195 sqft asking-rent advertisement](https://www.99.co/singapore/rent/property/east-meadows-condo-eJyJcru6RMu7m2HWB4PNke)
was marketed around S$4,900; that is an ask, not an achieved rent.

Using S$4,400 monthly rent against a S$1.52m purchase gives an indicative
**3.47% gross yield**. It excludes vacancy, maintenance, agent fees, repairs,
property tax, insurance, furnishing and financing. Rental fallback is useful,
but not strong enough to rescue an overpayment.

## Location and pipeline

Tanah Merah MRT is the main daily-use advantage. Walking time must be tested
from the exact block and gate, not inferred from a portal headline. Buyers
should also test East-West Line, aircraft and road noise at the exact stack
with windows open and closed, during peak and late-evening periods.

Pipeline is mixed:

- LTA brought the new Tanah Merah eastbound platform into service in December
  2025 as part of works for future airport connectivity:
  [LTA service and platform update](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2025/10/news-releases/train_service_adjustments_EWL_29Nov_to_8Dec25.html).

  station environment, but also add newer competing stock.
- URA awarded the 99-year
  [Bedok Rise residential site](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr25-66/)

  expectations for age and facilities.

  [Bayshore Drive mixed-use site](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr26-55/)

  not a direct valuation uplift for East Meadows.

## Underwriting and future quantum

The representative case is a normal approximately 1,195 sqft three-bedroom at
**S$1.52m**. Current residential BSD is **S$45,600**, using the
[IRAS BSD tiers](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29).
The illustration assumes dutiable value equals entry price; actual BSD and
ABSD apply to the higher of purchase consideration or market value.
The no-ABSD case assumes a Singapore citizen buying a first residential
property.

The formula is:

`capital-only profit = exit price × 0.975 − entry price − BSD − S$5,000 acquisition legal/technical allowance`

The 2.5% exit friction and fixed S$5,000 acquisition allowance are assumptions.
The table excludes any other legal fees, financing, maintenance, renovation,
property tax, rent, vacancy and opportunity cost. The second-property column
subtracts a further **20% of entry price**, reflecting the current
[Singapore-citizen second-residential-property ABSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29).

| Hold / annual price growth | Exit quantum | SC first-property capital-only profit | SC second-property after 20% ABSD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 years / 0% | S$1.520m | -S$89k | -S$393k |
| 5 years / 2% | S$1.678m | S$66k | -S$238k |
| 5 years / 3% | S$1.762m | S$147k | -S$157k |
| 5 years / 4% | S$1.849m | S$232k | -S$72k |
| 8 years / 0% | S$1.520m | -S$89k | -S$393k |
| 8 years / 2% | S$1.781m | S$166k | -S$138k |
| 8 years / 3% | S$1.925m | S$307k | S$3k |
| 8 years / 4% | S$2.080m | S$458k | S$154k |

The SC first-property break-even growth rate is about 1.2% annually over five
years or 0.7% over eight, before holding costs. Once real holding costs or
20% ABSD are included, the hurdle is materially higher. Under current
[SSD rules](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property),
a purchase on or after 4 July 2025 has a four-year SSD schedule; the modelled
five- and eight-year exits sit outside it if dates and rules are unchanged.

## Catalysts

- Short practical access to Tanah Merah MRT and established East-West Line
  connectivity.
- Family-sized floor plates at manageable absolute quantum relative to newer
  three-bedroom stock.
- Station-area retail and public-realm improvement as adjacent projects
  complete.
- A sufficiently discounted purchase can preserve resale optionality without
  requiring project-record PSF.

## Risks

- Lease age: approximately 70–71 years remain now and about 62–63 after an
  eight-year hold.
- Building-envelope and facility risk: lifts, façades, waterproofing, roofs,
  pipes, pool plant and carparks can create special levies or higher fees.
- Duplicate listing cards can exaggerate both availability and seller
  competition.
- Newer Tanah Merah supply can cap PSF convergence; do not assume East Meadows
  will rerate to a new launch on replacement-cost logic.
- Floor, rail, road and aircraft exposure can explain large same-size price
  dispersion.
- No current collective-sale tender was verified. En-bloc optionality carries
  zero value in the base case.

## Due diligence before an offer

1. Obtain the title, strata plan, exact floor plan and lease commencement date.
2. Review three years of MCST accounts and AGM minutes, the sinking-fund
   balance, planned major works and special levies.
3. Inspect water ingress, façade cracks, plumbing, air-conditioning ledges,
   lift performance and carpark condition.
4. Verify tenancy, vacant possession, renovation approvals and all disclosed
   defects.
5. Order a bank valuation and compare the subject with same-size,
   same-floor-band caveats. Offer from achieved evidence, not the seller's PSF
   comparison with a newer project.

## Final assessment

East Meadows works best as a **value-conscious own-stay or rental-fallback
purchase around S$1.50m–S$1.56m for a normal 1,195 sqft unit**. Liquidity is
adequate and the MRT proposition is durable. The realistic investment case is
moderate compounding plus a disciplined entry discount. Paying S$1.65m or more
for an ordinary unit converts that thesis into hope for premium future
pricing, which the current achieved evidence does not justify.
