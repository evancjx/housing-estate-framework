## Decision

Optima @ Tanah Merah is a practical near-MRT project with established rental
evidence, a manageable 297-home supply and useful conventional layouts. It has
**moderate defensive and selective appreciation potential**, but the data does
not support high-confidence "huge" future quantum at current asking prices.

The strongest balanced apparent lead is an accessible direct advertisement for
**1,001 sqft at S$1.70m**, about S$1,698 psf. A high-floor 1,001 sqft unit
achieved S$1.75m/S$1,748 psf in July 2026, while a mid-floor exact-size unit
sold for S$1.54m in October 2025. S$1.70m is therefore a sensible negotiation
anchor, not a major valuation error.

That S$1.70m page needs unusual care. It was not among the ten cards returned
by the current project search and stated a tenancy end date of 30 June 2026,
which had passed by the research date. It is evidence of an apparent seller
lead, not proof of present availability. The current search instead displayed
another 1,001 sqft ask at S$1.80m.

Evidence-backed buy boxes are:

1. **Best balanced lead:** 990–1,001 sqft around S$1.70m–S$1.75m, with floor
   and condition adjustments.
2. **Best established compact own-stay size:** 840–872 sqft around
   S$1.30m–S$1.40m when a genuine seller appears.
3. **Lowest-quantum rental ticket:** 484 sqft around S$800k–S$830k, but the
   last matching caveat is stale and bank valuation is essential.
4. **Larger conventional stock:** 1,259–1,303 sqft around
   S$2.20m–S$2.30m, not the S$2.35m–S$2.45m captured asks unless the unit has
   a material advantage.
5. **Special large/roof stock:** assess on internal usable area, strata plan
   and bank valuation, not low headline PSF.

The captured 700 sqft asks of S$1.25m–S$1.50m run 20%–43% above the latest
exact-size caveat from October 2024. The 1,098 sqft ask at S$1.86m is about 16%
above the latest exact-size caveat from December 2024. These are not attractive
merely because their total price is below larger family units.

Here, "quantum potential" means whether the total purchase basis permits a
credible future resale gain after acquisition and selling friction. Optima's
MRT proximity and lower total price are useful, but a 99-year lease commencing
in 2008 means a five- or eight-year exit also sells an older remaining lease.

## Scope and evidence rules

This is a property analysis, not a factor-audit report and not a unified
property ranking. It distinguishes:

- **advertised asks**, which may be duplicated, refreshed, stale or
  non-executable;
- **achieved resale caveats and rent contracts**, which are stronger evidence
  but do not disclose every unit attribute; and
- **scenario underwriting**, which is explicit arithmetic rather than a
  guaranteed outcome.

The primary achieved-price source is
[URA Property Market Information](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/property-market-information/pmiResidentialTransactionSearch).
Only rows classified as **Resale** are used for secondary-market liquidity.
Caveats are voluntary and are not exhaustive; URA's broader methodology is
described in
[REALIS coverage and methodology](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/reis/coverageandMethodology).

The exact-area ledger was reconciled against the public
[PropertyVow Optima transaction record](https://sg.propertyvow.com/property/private-property/detail/optima-at-tanah-merah)
and
[99.co Optima project record](https://www.99.co/singapore/condos-apartments/optima-tanah-merah).
Exact area, floor band and date are more reliable bid anchors than a portal
bedroom tag.

## Project proposition

The public project record describes Optima as a 297-home, six-block project by
TID, completed in 2012, with blocks at 78–88 Tanah Merah Kechil Avenue and up
to 14 storeys. Facilities include a lap pool, tennis court, clubhouse, fitness
area and playground. Tanah Merah MRT is described as roughly a two-minute walk.

URA-derived records report a **99-year lease commencing in 2008**, leaving
approximately **80–81 years** at the research date. On the illustrated five-
and eight-year exits, remaining tenure would be roughly 75–76 and 72–73 years
respectively. This is not an immediate mainstream-financing cliff, but it is a
relative disadvantage against Sceneca Residence's 2021 lease and will become
more visible with time.

Optima benefits from Sceneca Square's supermarket and retail without paying for
a brand-new unit. It also competes directly with Sceneca's newer, integrated
homes and with Urban Vista, Casa Merah and other D16 stock. The location offers
substitutes as well as demand.

## Five- and eight-year capital-only quantum test

The illustration uses a **S$1.70m purchase of 1,001 sqft**, the strongest
balanced apparent lead. It assumes the seller and price can be verified.
It assumes dutiable value equals the entry price; actual BSD and ABSD apply to
the higher of purchase consideration or market value.

Assumptions:

- BSD: S$54,600 under current residential brackets;
- purchase legal/valuation allowance: S$5,000;
- all-in selling friction: 2.5% of exit price, so net exit proceeds are 97.5%
  of gross exit;
- no rental income, mortgage interest, maintenance, property tax, insurance,
  vacancy or renovation;
- sale after the current SSD period; and
- Singapore citizen first-property basis of S$1,759,600, versus
  Singapore citizen second-property basis of S$2,099,600 after 20% ABSD.

[IRAS BSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29),

and

must generally be held for more than four years to avoid SSD. The five- and
eight-year tests assume complete holding periods and unchanged rules. Actual
ABSD depends on legal owner profile and any remission.

## Principal risks

- **Finite and ageing lease:** approximately 80–81 years remain now, with
  lower tenure on exit.
- **Newer direct competitor:** Sceneca offers a younger lease, integrated
  retail and direct link at the same station.
- **Exact-size evidence can be stale:** the 484, 700, 1,098 and 1,302 sqft
  segments lack recent matching caveats.
- **Floor and stack distort short-run comparisons:** the two latest 1,001 sqft
  transactions do not establish a 13.6% project growth rate.
- **Special-layout PSF trap:** roof terraces, voids and outdoor strata area can
  make very large homes look cheap per sqft while narrowing the buyer pool.
- **Ageing-asset costs:** review façade, waterproofing, lifts, mechanical
  systems, pool/tennis upkeep, sinking fund and possible special levies.
- **Rail and road externalities:** noise, announcements, privacy, traffic and
  west sun differ materially by block and stack.
- **Substitute and future supply:** several same-station projects and Bedok
  Rise limit pricing power.
- **Rental yield is not uniformly high:** the illustrated conventional
  1,001 sqft yield is below 3% gross at S$1.70m.
- **Portal staleness and duplication:** an accessible page is not proof of an
  available or executable seller.
- **Policy costs:** second-property ABSD makes ordinary appreciation
  insufficient for years, and SSD prevents a low-cost short flip.

## Due diligence before an option to purchase

For a 990–1,001 sqft candidate:

1. Confirm the exact unit, seller identity, same-day availability and written
   executable price. Resolve why the S$1.70m page is absent from current
   project search results.
2. Obtain a bank valuation and same-stack/floor caveats. Do not apply the July
   2026 high-floor S$1.75m record automatically to a low-floor unit.
3. Verify vacant possession. The S$1.70m page's stated 30 June 2026 tenancy end
   has passed; obtain handover records or any renewed tenancy.
4. Inspect peak/off-peak MRT and road noise, west sun, privacy, view,
   ventilation and potential obstruction.
5. Review the strata plan, measured internal usable area, balcony/planter/void
   components and all approved alterations.
6. Review MCST maintenance, sinking fund, AGM minutes, insurance, lift and
   façade works, waterproofing, defects, litigation and special levies.
7. Check title, caveats, encumbrances, seller acquisition date and any seller
   SSD constraint.
8. If tenanted, sight the full tenancy, deposit, payment history, inventory,
   repair duties and diplomatic/break clauses.
9. Model buyer-specific ABSD, TDSR, loan-to-value, interest-rate stress,
   renovation, vacancy and a holding period beyond SSD.

For 1,959 sqft or other roof/penthouse stock, also obtain the strata plan,
internal-versus-outdoor area measurement, waterproofing history, exclusive-use
rights, roof responsibility and exact-layout valuation comparables.

## Final assessment

Optima @ Tanah Merah has **moderate defensive quality, reasonable but not deep
liquidity, and moderate selective appreciation potential**. MRT proximity,
established rentals, efficient conventional stock and Sceneca Square's retail
support make it practical to own and let.

The evidence does not support project-wide huge quantum potential. At
S$1.70m, a 1,001 sqft purchase is below the latest high-floor exact-size
caveat, but gross rental fallback is only about 2.8%. A S$500,000 capital-only
profit requires approximately S$2.318m/S$2,315 psf and 6.4% annual price growth
for five years. S$1m requires approximately S$2.830m/S$2,828 psf and 10.7%
annual five-year growth. Neither is an appropriate base-case assumption.

The rational strategy is to target a verified conventional layout at or below
matching caveats, insist on floor/stack adjustments, and avoid paying a
new-project-style premium for an older lease. Special large units can suit an
own-stayer, but their low headline PSF should not be confused with broad resale
liquidity or efficient usable area.

This report is a point-in-time research snapshot, not valuation, legal, tax or
financial advice. Advertisements, caveats, rents, supply plans and tax rules
can change after the capture timestamp and must be refreshed before an offer.
