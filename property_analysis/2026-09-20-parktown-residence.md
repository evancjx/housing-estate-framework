## Decision

**Treat Parktown as a selective purchase of remaining integrated family stock; the decision is whether the exact layout earns its total price and wait, not whether the launch sold well.**

At the S$2,723,000 historical entry illustration, an unchanged exit price loses S$178,825; a 10% higher exit leaves +S$86,668 and a 10% lower exit leaves −S$444,318 before financing, ongoing property tax, maintenance, renovation and rent. These are total price changes with no forecast date. Own-stay utility can justify a purchase, but it should not be presented as a demonstrated investment return.

The captured official 2026Q2 rental series does not establish a current subject rental median for this comparison. Rental fallback is unproven here; a missing statistic is neither zero rent nor a licence to substitute a neighbour's rent.

- URA developer return for 2026-08: 1,167 sold of 1,193 total homes, leaving 26 by arithmetic; 0 reported sold in that month. This is a dated aggregate balance, not an executable unit list.
- The recent >100–130 sqm New Sale cohort is S$2.723m median (N=52); the >130 sqm cohort is S$3.422m (N=14). A buyer cannot assume the smaller launch formats remain at historical prices.
- Its integrated facilities serve 1,193 homes and link to a future Tampines North MRT station. Future convenience also arrives with many potential owner-sale and rental substitutes.

## Purchase and exit economics

The common scenario is a Singapore citizen's first residential purchase with no ABSD, entry price equal to dutiable market value, current BSD, S$5,000 acquisition allowance and 2.5% selling costs. Actual duty uses the higher of price and market value. The exit must be permitted and outside the applicable SSD period and any EC MOP. Loan interest, CPF obligations, renovation, ongoing property tax, maintenance, vacancy, rent and opportunity cost are excluded.
