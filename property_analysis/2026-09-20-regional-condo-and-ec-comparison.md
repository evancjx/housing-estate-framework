## Decision

**Start with completed-home alternatives at the required floor area, then decide whether a newer lease, layout, location or integrated development earns its additional purchase and holding cost.** There is no single winning region across all budgets and unit sizes. There are several defensible purchase paths, and the evidence supporting them differs.

For a space-led household, the strongest starting points in this sample are older Simei/Tampines private homes and older Jurong EC-origin developments. Recent >100–130 sqm resale cohorts have medians of **S$1.250m at Melville Park, S$1.373m at Eastpoint Green, S$1.300m at Summerdale and S$1.460m at The Floravale**. These prices buy materially more registered area per dollar than the new launches. The trade-off is an older lease and building; the price difference must fund renovation, future maintenance and an acceptable eventual exit, rather than being treated as free upside.

For households wanting a more recent completed product, compare **Treasure at Tampines, The Glades / Grandeur Park Residences, The Commodore, Lake Grande / Lakeville, and Daintree / Verdale** at the exact required area. Their owner transactions provide an observable resale market. They still need different allowances for location, lease, layout and condition, so this is a set of regional controls rather than a pooled ranking.

For new launches, **Canberra Crescent, Parktown, Pinery, LakeGarden, Sora, The Sen, Vela Bay and Dunearn House require a separate own-stay and investment decision**. New-sale demand demonstrates acceptance of the developer's product and payment schedule. It does not demonstrate that an owner can resell at a profit after stamp duty, carrying costs and competition from subsequent launches. A historical launch price is also not the price of the remaining homes.

The official end-August stock return makes that distinction concrete: **LakeGarden had three homes unsold by arithmetic, Parktown 26, Sora 211 and The Sen 192**. A near-sold project's remaining unit is a different decision from a project still carrying substantial primary stock. The cost test is equally material: at Pinery's S$3.059m larger-home cohort anchor, a 10% higher exit price leaves only about **S$94k before holding costs**, while a 10% lower exit loses about **S$503k**. New-launch enthusiasm is not a substitute for entry discipline.

| Purchase question | Preferred research path | What could change the conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most space at roughly S$1.25m–S$1.5m | Older completed Simei/Tampines homes and Summerdale / The Floravale; compare East Meadows in Bedok | Poor building condition, substantial outdoor/void area, unaffordable major works or an unsuitable remaining lease |
| A newer family home around S$1.5m–S$2m | Canberra owner-sale controls and eligible EC resales; selected Tampines and Jurong completed cohorts | An exact-unit premium unsupported by comparable caveats, or restrictions that make the proposed purchase unavailable |
| Rail/retail convenience rather than maximum area | Compare the project's actual walking route or integration with a completed alternative; pay only for utility the household will use | Treating a future station as operating today, or comparing an integrated project with a less convenient peer without acknowledging the difference |
| Freehold ownership | Separate compact Forett / The Creek evidence from larger Signature Park and premium Bukit Timah homes | Assuming freehold removes building-age, maintenance or resale-liquidity risks |
| Rental-led investment | Begin with completed projects with both owner transactions and achieved rental evidence | A gross yield that disappears after vacancy, tax, maintenance and financing; no subject rent before completion |
| A new EC | Check current developer stock and purchaser eligibility first; use private launches only as alternatives, not interchangeable comparables | Sell-out, MOP restrictions or a tender-date policy cohort that prevents the intended exit |

The result is an **entry-price and product selection analysis**, not a forecast that the cheapest PSF will appreciate fastest. Regional development plans are relevant to convenience and competing supply. None receives an automatic capital-growth allowance.

Four evidence classes remain distinct:

- **Achieved transactions:** URA New Sale, Sub Sale and Resale records, preserving the seller category and source tenure description. A sale classification does not prove current completion or available stock.
- **Project and supply facts:** dated government, developer and corporate disclosures. Estimated completion, expected possession and actual TOP are different facts.

Public resale and subsale coverage is caveat-based and not exhaustive. [URA coverage and methodology](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/reis/coverageandMethodology).

Three conclusions change the buying approach:

1. **A small remaining balance changes the product being bought.** Parktown, Pinery and LakeGarden can have attractive historical compact-unit prices while only materially different homes remain. Their broad cohort medians must not be used as a promise that a similar home is available.
2. **A large balance creates a different exit risk.** Sora's 211-home and The Sen's 192-home arithmetic balances mean a future owner may compete with the developer's payment terms, incentives and new-unit stock. This is a reason for entry discipline, not proof of an imminent discount.
3. **Launch age and release strategy matter.** Dunearn's 153 unsold homes include 130 not yet launched by August. Comparing its absolute balance with a project several years into sales would overstate what the number says about demand. One weak month also does not establish a durable sales trend.

The cumulative `sold_to_date` field controls this arithmetic. Reported monthly sales do not always sum to its changes, and cumulative totals can revise down. No cancellation or failed purchase is attributed to a particular home without separate evidence. New Sale transaction records are developer OTP evidence; owner Sub Sale and Resale records are caveat-based. Neither is a full-unit live inventory.

These broad-band rows do not establish a precise premium: their area distributions differ. They do establish the household decision. The current evidence does not support “all Tampines homes are rising together.” Within identical project/tenure/sale-type/size-band definitions, Arc's >70–100 sqm median PSF was approximately flat against the preceding year, while CityLife's >100–130 sqm median was about 7% higher and Treasure's about 9% higher. These are **composition-sensitive median changes**, not individual-owner appreciation. They are a reason to examine exact-area and floor evidence, not extrapolate a regional growth rate.

The existing [Pinery analysis](https://evancjx.github.io/housing-estate-framework/property-analysis-2026-08-08-pinery-residences.html) documents the planned direct underground Tampines West MRT connection and the distinction between the brochure's possession date and portal TOP estimates. The [Parktown analysis](https://evancjx.github.io/housing-estate-framework/property-analysis-2026-08-08-parktown-residence.html) explains the integrated product and high-quantum residual formats. Their August advertised stock must be rechecked before use. Rivelle's developer currently reports the EC fully sold; its historical price is not an available entry point. [Sim Lian: Rivelle](https://www.simlian.com.sg/property/rivelletampines/).

**Entry discipline:** use Melville/Eastpoint/Arc to test how much space the budget buys; use Treasure to test the premium for a later-lease completed home; then require Parktown or Pinery to justify their additional quantum through the exact layout, access and holding horizon. Do not finance an assumed future integration premium twice, once in the purchase price and again in the exit forecast.

## Narrower owner-price controls: where the entry premium needs an explanation

The broad regional tables answer the budget question. This second pass narrows **both the subject transactions and owner-sale controls** to the same area window around the original subject cohort's median: ±5%, with a minimum tolerance of 3 sqm. It requires at least five transactions on each side, the same region, the same private/EC-origin category and tenure group. Where both 99-year lease starts are known, they must be within ten years. Resale and Sub Sale remain separate.

Known project-point distances above 2 km are rejected. A missing coordinate produces an explicitly regional comparison, not an invented nearby peer. The figures below have matched points within 2 km, but that is **straight-line project proximity, not equal station access or walking distance**. Floor, facing, actual building age, internal layout, condition and incentives remain unadjusted.

Project proximity was not verified in this automated match; access, facilities, condition and layout can explain part of the difference. It is not evidence that Arc must catch up.

## Rental fallback: achieved rent must support the purchase price

Official **2026Q2 project rental medians** are available for **119 of the 553 main-scope project names**. Missing current-quarter rent remains missing; an older observation is not silently substituted and a blank is not zero demand. The rental source reports project-level monthly PSF, not rent matched to the exact sale unit or size cohort. [URA Data Service rental medians](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/maps/api/).

It is useful for deciding what to investigate, but it is not a matched-unit achievable yield or net return. All sale rows in this table are private Resales in the September 2025–August 2026 window.

**The investment implication is not “buy the highest yield.”** Melville's stronger gross ratio may be compensation for lease age, condition or capital expenditure. J Gateway's higher rent does not automatically beat Lake Grande's return once the purchase price is included. Forett's freehold ownership preference must be distinguished from the income generated per purchase dollar.

For a construction-stage purchase with no subject rent, the rental fallback is **unproven**. A completed neighbour's rent is a scenario input requiring an explicit adjustment for unit size, layout, location and delivery date. It must not appear as achieved rent for Canberra Crescent, Pinery, Parktown, LakeGarden, Sora, The Sen, Vela Bay or Dunearn House. Their construction period can also produce interest and accommodation costs before any rent is possible.

For scale, a S$2.4m purchase needs **S$7,000 per month** for a 3.5% gross yield; a S$3m purchase needs **S$8,750**. That arithmetic is a rental hurdle, not an estimate of what a tenant will pay. One vacant month alone removes one-twelfth of scheduled annual rent, before property tax, MCST fees, repairs, letting costs, insurance and financing. A project with insufficient official rent evidence remains a conditional investment case, not a zero-yield or automatically poor property.

## Entry and exit economics: price growth is not the owner's profit

The common illustration assumes a **Singapore-citizen first residential purchase, no ABSD, current BSD, S$5,000 acquisition allowance and 2.5% selling friction**. Entry price is assumed equal to dutiable market value; actual stamp duty uses the higher of consideration and market value. Exit is assumed legally permitted and outside applicable SSD and EC MOP. Financing, CPF obligations, renovation, maintenance, ongoing property tax, insurance, vacancy and rent are excluded.

These are **hypothetical purchases at historical cohort medians**, not current asks or real units. The PSF calculation uses the cohort's median price and median area, which may come from different records. It can therefore differ from the independently observed median transaction PSF. The appendix labels this synthetic anchor explicitly.

The 10% is a **total exit-price change, not annual growth**, and there is no assumed date at which it occurs. The S$500,000 column is a common hurdle for comparison, not an objective attributed to the buyer. Current [IRAS BSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29) and [SSD rules](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property) support the tax treatment; the allowances are research assumptions.

**The key finding is the asymmetry after friction.** At the S$3.059m Pinery anchor, a 10% rise leaves about S$94k before holding costs, while a 10% fall loses about **S$503k**. At the S$1.250m Melville anchor, the corresponding outcomes are approximately **+S$51k and −S$193k**. Lower entry quantum reduces the absolute money exposed, but does not establish a better building or a faster growth rate.

For entry discipline, invert the test: **maximum entry plus its BSD and acquisition cost must fit within the conservative exit proceeds**. If a buyer's base case requires a completed peer's price to jump materially simply to recover costs, the purchase is growth-dependent. If the home delivers enough own-stay utility to justify that dependence, say so explicitly rather than presenting it as an income investment.

For a Singapore citizen buying a second residential property without remission, current 20% ABSD materially changes the result. At a S$1.5m entry, capital-only break-even rises from about **S$1.589m to S$1.897m** before holding costs. That is a tax sensitivity, not a determination of purchaser eligibility, and it is not applied to new-EC purchase illustrations. [IRAS ABSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29).

A September 2026 private-home purchase also falls under the four-year SSD regime for acquisitions from 4 July 2025. Selling at TOP does not automatically mean the SSD period has ended. New ECs have a separate and potentially much longer restriction, so a common five-year investment exit cannot be imposed across this comparison.

## What the data can and cannot decide

The broad scan can identify an area's price/space trade-offs, expose a large new-sale premium, locate completed owner-sale controls, and show where an investment case depends on unverified rent or future demand. It cannot decide whether a particular unit has an acceptable stack, sound condition, efficient internal area, affordable MCST liabilities, a willing seller or a supportive bank valuation.

For each shortlisted home, the purchase decision needs the **exact registered area and floor plan, floor and facing, tenure/lease, executable price, transaction costs, realistic rent where relevant and a holding horizon compatible with the property and buyer**. A school-distance claim requires the actual block/address and current official check; a project-centroid circle is not admission eligibility. [OneMap SchoolQuery](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/school), [MOE home-address guidance](https://www.moe.gov.sg/primary/p1-registration/home-address).

The following exclusions and uncertainty controls are material:

- Four bulk transactions remain in the source ledger but are excluded from single-home metrics: Watten Estate Condominium, Bagnall Court, Lakeside Apartments and Park View Mansion. Redevelopment predecessors are not merged into their replacements.
- 154 generic-name records across the downloaded districts have unresolved geographic/project identity. They are retained separately and are not silently allocated to the 553 names.
- Exact source tenure strings remain separate, including generic leasehold entries. The Hillford's 60-year tenure is not pooled with ordinary 99-year or freehold homes.
- A period-to-period median change requires at least ten transactions in each same-project, exact-tenure, sale-type and size-band cell. Even then it is a descriptive mix-sensitive change, not a repeat-sale return.
- Transaction counts are evidence depth, not selling time or stock turnover. Verified total units and listing exposure are not available consistently for all projects.
- Completion is unverified where no actual TOP source was found. New Sale does not automatically mean under construction; zero Resales does not prove no private owner transaction ever occurred.
- No school-quality rank, investment forecast or unified Provision/Liveability/Value score is added. The repository's objective Provision and person-relative Liveability concepts remain separate.
