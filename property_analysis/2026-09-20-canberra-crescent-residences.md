**The 13 August classification stands: moderate/selective potential quantum, not huge potential quantum.** Five weeks of new evidence tighten rather than loosen it.

The official-source ledger has advanced from 340 to **347 New Sales** (92.3% of 376), with **no Sub Sale or Resale**. All seven caveats added since the last capture are **1,216 sq ft four-bedroom Standard homes** at **S$2.38m–S$2.488m / S$1,957–S$2,046 psf**. The 990 sq ft three-bedroom Premium format, which the previous report named the safer liquidity screen, has recorded **no caveat since June 2026** and has the largest residual count. Buyers who are still arriving are choosing the larger format at a slightly lower PSF.

The premium over completed substitutes has not narrowed. Size-matched 2026 evidence puts the subject's three-bedroom-sized homes at **S$2,028 psf** against **S$1,731 psf** for The Watergardens at Canberra sub-sales and **S$1,661 psf** for The Commodore's first resales. Watergardens' annual sub-sale median has slipped from **S$1,771 (2024) to S$1,745 (2025) to S$1,720 psf (2026)**.

For a buyer entering today at the captured residual asks, the capital-only break-even after BSD and selling friction is **S$2,113–S$2,138 psf**. That is above **every** 2026 District 27 non-subject transaction; the highest was a North Park Residences resale at S$2,039 psf. A S$500,000 capital-only gain needs **S$2,526–S$2,651 psf**.

**SSD timing.** A purchase in September 2026 is inside the four-year SSD period until September 2030. Expected vacant possession is April 2030. To reach the S$500,000 target, the required exit PSF implies about **6.0%–7.2% a year** to September 2030 from today's ask. It needs the district's price ceiling to move roughly 24%–30% above North Park's 2026 high. That is a bull case, not current evidence.

## Key risks

- **No secondary proof.** Zero Sub Sales or Resales after 13 months; the entry-to-exit spread is unknown.
- **Break-even above the district ceiling.** Residual asks need about S$2,113–S$2,138 psf to break even, and no 2026 District 27 owner sale has exceeded S$2,039 psf.
- **Stalled 990 sq ft format.** No caveat since June; future owners of this format will resell into a format the developer is finding slow to clear.
- **Peer drift.** Watergardens sub-sale medians have fallen for two consecutive years. The Commodore's first resales sit about 22% below the subject.
- **Direct private competitor.** Chencharu Close (~875 homes) is still to launch.
- **Tenant headwinds at completion.** The shipyard exits in 2028, the RTS opens in 2027, and 376 homes are handed over together.
- **SSD overlaps handover.** A September 2026 buyer is SSD-liable until September 2030, after the April 2030 expected possession.
- **Carry risk.** Progressive payments and interest accrue for about 3.5 years before any rent.
- **Delivery-date ambiguity.** Portals show 2028–2030; only the brochure's April 2030 expected possession and the executed agreement should be relied on.
- **Public-feed risk.** Agent balances and price lists lag reservations, cancellations and options.

## Buyer verification checklist

The 12-point checklist in the 13 August report still applies in full. Additions for this capture:

1. Ask the developer to confirm whether the September residual list includes any 990 sq ft incentive or reprice. A stalled format is where negotiation room, if any, will appear.
2. For a 1,216 sq ft home, confirm floor, stack and PES status. The August S$2.38m ground-floor caveat suggests the cheapest variant has probably gone.
3. Stress the exit at **S$1,720–S$1,906 psf** (The Watergardens/Commodore to North Park 2026 levels). Proceed only if a loss of S$0.2m–S$0.5m would not impair household finances.
4. Model SSD explicitly if there is any chance of selling between April and September 2030.
5. Check Chencharu Close's launch pricing before committing. If it launches materially above S$2,000 psf, the subject's relative value improves; if near it, the subject faces a newer, better-located alternative.
6. Do not rely on an RTS-driven rental story; stress rent at URA's Watergardens 25th percentile (S$4.20 psf per month, about S$4,160 on a 990 sq ft home).

Canberra Crescent Residences continues to sell, but slowly and only in its larger formats. Official-source caveats reached **347 / 376 (92.3%)** by 19 September 2026, and all seven new caveats since mid-August are 1,216 sq ft four-bedroom homes near S$2,030 psf. No owner has yet sold, and no 990 sq ft three-bedroom home has been caveated since June.

The project remains a credible own-stay product: new, fully facilitated, near Canberra Plaza and Bukit Canberra, on a competitive S$793 psf ppr land basis. The capital case has not improved. Close completed substitutes clear near S$1,720 psf, and the best-located district comparable peaks near S$2,040 psf. The subject's residual stock is priced about S$2,000 psf, needs about S$2,113–S$2,138 psf to break even after costs, and faces Chencharu Close, a steady EC pipeline and tenant-side headwinds around its 2030 completion.

**Classification: moderate/selective potential quantum, not high-confidence huge potential quantum.** For an owner-occupier with a long horizon who values a new northern condominium, a 1,216 sq ft home at or below the S$2.41m mid-ask is the more rational residual choice than the 990 sq ft at upper-floor asks. For an investor, the arithmetic does not work at current prices.
