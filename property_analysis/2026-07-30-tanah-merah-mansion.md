## Decision

Tanah Merah Mansion has **two separate investment sub-markets**:

- ordinary approximately 872–1,324 sqft apartments, with recent February
  2025–January 2026 evidence at S$1,135–S$1,178 psf and a wider 2024–2026
  range of S$1,106–S$1,259 psf; and
- one of only four reported duplex homes around 3,300–3,600 sqft, where PSF is
  much lower but absolute quantum, renovation and liquidity risk are much
  higher.

The current 1,324 sqft S$1.56m advertisement exactly matches the rounded area,
price and PSF of the January 2026 caveat. It is broadly supportable, but the
similarity also raises a stale-card or post-sale-data question: confirm that it
is a different live unit or that the prior transaction did not already close
the marketed property.

The 3,348 sqft duplex was scheduled for an **owner's sale at auction on
29 July 2026** with a S$3.25m guide. No authoritative public result was found
by this report's 30 July capture. It must not be called sold, unsold or still
executable without SRI's written confirmation. A separate 3,482 sqft card asks
about S$3.50m.

The format-specific entry discipline is:

| Format | Potentially compelling | Defensible | Requires exceptional evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ordinary 1,195–1,324 sqft | At or below S$1.45m | S$1.45m–S$1.56m | Above S$1.56m |
| Duplex approximately 3,348 sqft | At or below S$3.10m | S$3.10m–S$3.30m | Above S$3.30m; especially S$3.50m |

The duplex is the more plausible **large absolute-dollar quantum** thesis. A
representative S$3.25m purchase has a friction-only basis of S$3.3896m and
requires about S$3.989m at exit for S$500,000 capital-only profit. That needs
only about 4.18% annual sale-price growth over five years or 2.60% over eight,
but those rates exclude a potentially major renovation, financing, maintenance,
property tax and the cost of waiting for an uncommon next buyer.

For an ordinary S$1.56m home, the same S$500,000 target requires an exit near
S$2.167m—about 6.79% annual growth over five years or 4.19% over eight.

The conclusion is therefore **moderate-to-high conditional duplex optionality,
but not high-confidence huge risk-adjusted quantum**. The low duplex PSF is
real achieved context, yet enormous area, a 1984 building and scarce buyer pool
can keep PSF low.

more recent February 2025–January 2026 observations form a tighter

That supports fair-market status, not a bargain. A target below S$1.50m
creates room for:

- common-property and unit repairs;
- an older-building valuation haircut;
- sale friction; and
- competition from newer, more efficient facilities.

The ordinary format provides a broader next-buyer and rental pool than a
duplex, but also less absolute-dollar optionality.

## Duplex valuation and optionality

The duplex's low PSF can mean:

- genuine space value;
- inefficient stairs, voids, balconies or circulation;
- high renovation and air-conditioning cost;
- a walk-up or access constraint that must be physically checked;
- a small family buyer pool; or
- some combination.

The S$3.25m guide is broadly anchored by the S$3.189m 2023 achieved quantum.
A prudent duplex offer must separately budget:

- professional inspection and specialist waterproofing review;
- full or partial renovation;
- electrical, plumbing and air-conditioning capacity;
- moving and temporary accommodation;
- maintenance and property tax; and
- a longer expected marketing period at exit.

No collective-sale premium is included. Freehold, 36 homes and an old building
do not establish owner consent, planning yield, developer appetite, successful
tender or net proceeds.

## Capital-only return tests

Both tests assume:

- **Singapore citizen (SC) buying a first residential property**, the SC
  first-property baseline with no ABSD;
- S$5,000 acquisition legal/technical allowance;
- 97.5% exit proceeds after 2.5% selling friction; and
- no financing, renovation, maintenance, tax, vacancy or rent in profit.

BSD follows
[current IRAS residential tiers](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29).

Each scenario assumes dutiable value equals its stated entry price. Actual BSD
and ABSD are charged on the **higher of consideration or market value**, so an
apparently discounted purchase can attract duty on a higher value.

### Ordinary-unit illustration

| Input | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Purchase price | S$1.560m |
| BSD | S$47,600 |
| Acquisition legal/technical allowance | S$5,000 |
| Acquisition basis | **S$1.6126m** |
| Friction-only break-even exit | **S$1.654m** |
| Exit needed for S$500,000 capital-only profit | **S$2.167m** |

`ordinary capital-only profit = 0.975 × exit price − S$1.6126m`

| Annual nominal sale-price change | Five-year exit / profit | Eight-year exit / profit |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1% | S$1.640m / **−S$14k** | S$1.689m / **S$34k** |
| 3% | S$1.808m / **S$151k** | S$1.976m / **S$314k** |
| 5% | S$1.991m / **S$329k** | S$2.305m / **S$635k** |

| 5% | S$4.148m / **S$655k** | S$4.802m / **S$1.292m** |

The duplex table exposes why absolute quantum can be large and still not be
risk-adjusted “huge potential”: an unmodelled six-figure renovation, financing
and holding cost can materially reduce every profit row.

[IRAS's current ABSD table](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29)
imposes 20% for a Singapore citizen buying a second residential property.
That adds S$312,000 to the ordinary example or S$650,000 to the duplex. It
raises break-even to about **S$1.974m** and **S$4.143m**, respectively, before
holding costs.

For purchases on or after 4 July 2025,
[IRAS's current SSD schedule](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property)
applies 16%, 12%, 8% and 4% within the first four holding years. The five- and
eight-year cases assume sale after the exact fourth anniversary and no SSD
under current rules.

## Due diligence before an offer

1. Obtain written post-29-July status from SRI for the 3,348 sqft duplex:
   auction result, current seller authority, executable price and terms.
2. Reconcile every card to a unique subsidiary strata lot; identify duplicates,
   stale cards and the relation between the 3,348 and 3,482 sqft homes.
3. Match title, strata plan, approved floor plan, share value and option;
   separate enclosed area, stairs, voids, balconies, terraces and roof space.
4. Commission a specialist inspection for waterproofing, roof, windows,
   plumbing, electrical capacity, structure, air-conditioning and prior
   alterations.
5. Obtain detailed renovation quotations and contingency before treating low
   PSF as a discount.
6. Request AGM minutes, audited accounts, maintenance and sinking funds,
   insurance, arrears, disputes and planned special levies.
7. Confirm lift/access conditions, stair and fire-safety compliance, parking,
   security, refuse route and delivery practicality for the exact unit.
8. Verify occupancy, tenancy, deposits, inventory, rent and vacant possession
   in writing.
9. Obtain an independent bank valuation and loan indication for the exact
   uncommon duplex format.
10. Recompute BSD, ABSD, SSD, CPF and financing for the actual buyers and
    contract date; model holding, renovation and exit time separately.
11. Walk both future Bedok South and Tanah Merah station routes from the gate
    and visit at rain, peak, school and late-night periods.
12. Assign zero value to en-bloc speculation unless a current, consented,
    professionally supported exercise and net-proceeds analysis exist.

## Final assessment

The ordinary S$1.56m home is fair-market inventory, not an obvious quantum
mispricing. The duplex around S$3.10m–S$3.30m is more interesting: it is close
to a 2023 achieved total quantum and can produce substantial absolute upside
under moderate long-run growth. Yet the unknown auction result, enormous
renovation exposure, 1984 common property, low rent and rare exit buyer make
the opportunity conditional. Verify the physical and legal asset first; do
not buy the low PSF headline.
