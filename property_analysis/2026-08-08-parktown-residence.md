# PARKTOWN Residence — remaining-inventory quantum and exit-potential analysis

Research captured: **2026-08-08 02:25:47 SGT (UTC+08:00)**  
Property: **PARKTOWN Residence, 1–23 Tampines Street 62, Singapore**  
Analysis type: **property new-launch inventory, matched-peer valuation and investment analysis**  
Status: **point-in-time market snapshot**  
Market stage: **new launch**  
Summary: **The low-quantum 4-Bedroom Premium stock is the most defensible remaining buy; capital upside is plausible but entry-dependent, and there is no achieved owner-resale premium yet.**

## Decision

PARKTOWN Residence is a strong integrated own-stay product, but the remaining
2026 inventory is **tail stock at high absolute quantum**, not an obvious
large-capital-gain trade.

The best risk-adjusted choice is a **4-Bedroom Premium around S$3.32m–S$3.42m**,
subject to an acceptable valuation, efficient strata area and a stack that is
not impaired by patio, privacy, road, retail-service or construction exposure.
The August price chart's cheapest 1,496 sqft unit is a second-floor patio type
at S$3.322m, while the cheapest ordinary DP2 type is S$3.418m. The latter is a
cleaner underwriting anchor. A purchase above about **S$3.50m** needs a
materially better floor or facing because captured 2026 achieved new-sale
evidence for the same official 4-Bedroom Premium area band had a S$3.341m
median and S$3.570m top.

The **5-Bedroom is defensible for multigenerational own stay around
S$3.86m–S$3.94m**, but its narrower buyer pool and higher carrying cost make it
less attractive as a pure investment. The S$4.151m high-floor ask exceeds the
highest S$4.048m achieved 5-Bedroom transaction in the captured project
extract and leaves little price-discovery cushion.

| Entry question | Captured answer |
|---|---|
| Is there proven owner-resale profit? | **No.** All 1,165 captured project rows through July 2026 are New Sale; no Sub Sale or Resale row is present. |
| Is the project itself well accepted? | **Yes.** 1,041 units sold on launch weekend at S$2,360 psf average; 1,116 were booked by 31 December 2025. |
| Is the cheapest remaining 4BR quantum supportable? | **Reasonably, but stack-dependent.** S$2,221–S$2,285 psf is near/below recent same-project large-unit evidence and below Pinery's matched large-unit new-sale median. |
| Is there “huge” capital potential? | **Not on conservative assumptions.** Friction-only break-even is about 7% above entry before financing and ownership costs. |
| Best buyer profile | Long-horizon own-stay family that values integration and can hold beyond completion and SSD. |
| Weakest buyer profile | Yield-led or short-hold investor relying on the 2030 MRT opening to create an immediate premium. |

At the cheapest captured developer asks, a capital-only five-year scenario
produces about **S$110k–S$119k** after BSD, S$5k acquisition allowance and
2.5% exit friction if prices grow 2% annually. At 4% annual growth, the same
illustration produces approximately **S$475k–S$542k**. Those are scenarios,
not forecasts, and they exclude loan interest, progressive-payment interest,
maintenance, property tax, insurance, renovation and ABSD.

The conclusion is therefore **buy selectively for utility first; treat capital
gain as moderate and entry-price dependent**. Do not pay the top of the
remaining range merely because the MRT, mall and transport hub are described
as integrated: at capture, the Cross Island Line station is future rather
than operating, and much of that eventual convenience is already embedded in
the project's launch positioning.

## Transport, schools and completion timing

### Transport now versus later

The [official PARKTOWN launch release](https://www.capitaland.com/en/about-capitaland/newsroom/news-releases/international/2025/february/cld-unveil-parktown-residence-largest-fully-integrated-development-in-sg.html)

Tampines Link and Tampines Avenue 11 and that
[CRL Phase 1 passenger service is slated for 2030](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2022/2/news-releases/LTA_awards_civil_contracts_for_design_and_construction.html).
The [current LTA CRL project page](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/upcoming_projects/rail_expansion/cross_island_line.html)
continues to target CRL1 completion in 2030.

At capture, CR6 is not operating. A single-point Haversine check against the
repository's operational-station layer gives approximately 2.22km to
Tampines EWL, 2.37km to Tampines West DTL and 2.53km to Pasir Ris EWL. These
are straight-line distances, not walking routes. Present-day buyers therefore
underwrite bus/road access until both project and station are delivered.

The [URA Master Plan 2025 east-region page](https://www.ura.gov.sg/land-planning/master-plan/master-plan-2025/regional-plans/east/transforming-towns-for-tomorrow/)
supports the broader Tampines North plan: Tampines Boulevard Park, an
integrated transport hub, upcoming Tampines North MRT, hawker centre,
community club and new amenities. This is a genuine planning catalyst, but
delivery dates and resale willingness to pay are outcomes, not guaranteed
premiums.

### Schools

1km**. The repository's single project-point Haversine calculation is about

## Capital-potential model

### Cost assumptions

The scenario uses:

- current [IRAS residential BSD bands](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29):
  1%, 2%, 3%, 4%, 5%, then 6% above S$3m;
- S$5,000 acquisition legal/valuation allowance;
- 2.5% of exit price for negotiated selling commission, GST and legal
  allowance; CEA confirms that
  [commission rates are not fixed](https://www.cea.gov.sg/consumers/engaging-a-property-agent/what-to-take-note-of-when-engaging-a-property-agent/);
- no ABSD, financing, progressive interest, maintenance, property tax,
  insurance, renovation, vacancy or opportunity cost; and
- sale after the applicable SSD window.

ABSD is buyer-specific and can radically change the result; verify it using
the [current IRAS ABSD rules](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29).
A purchase exercised on or after 4 July 2025 falls under the revised
[four-year residential SSD schedule](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property):
16%, 12%, 8% and 4% within years one through four respectively. By contrast,
original February 2025 buyers fall under the earlier three-year schedule.
Confirm the exact acquisition/disposal dates with a conveyancing lawyer.

### Friction-only break-even

`capital profit = exit price × 97.5% − entry price − BSD − S$5,000`

| Advertised entry anchor | BSD | Friction-only break-even exit | Gross uplift required |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| S$3.322m 4BR patio low | S$138,920 | S$3.555m | 7.0% |
| S$3.418m 4BR ordinary low | S$144,680 | S$3.659m | 7.1% |
| S$3.858m 5BR patio low | S$171,080 | S$4.138m | 7.2% |
| S$3.937m 5BR ordinary low | S$175,820 | S$4.223m | 7.3% |
| S$4.151m 5BR high ask | S$188,660 | S$4.456m | 7.3% |

These thresholds still exclude every holding and financing cost. True
economic break-even will be higher.

### Five- and eight-year scenarios

The horizon runs from the August 2026 purchase assumption. “Flat”, 2% and 4%
are annual sale-price paths, not expected returns. Numbers are rounded to the
nearest S$1,000.

|---|---|---:|---:|
| S$3.322m 4BR patio | 5 years / flat | S$3.322m | **−S$227k** |

| S$3.322m 4BR patio | 5 years / 4% p.a. | S$4.042m | **+S$475k** |
| S$3.322m 4BR patio | 8 years / 2% p.a. | S$3.892m | **+S$329k** |
| S$3.858m 5BR patio | 5 years / flat | S$3.858m | **−S$273k** |
| S$3.858m 5BR patio | 5 years / 2% p.a. | S$4.260m | **+S$119k** |

The optimistic result comes from compounding a large base, but so does the
downside. A flat resale market creates a S$227k–S$273k capital-only loss even
before holding costs. The cheapest patio units also require a unit-specific
discount if their strata area or privacy is less useful than an ordinary
layout, so they cannot automatically be treated as the best value.

## Catalysts

- **Integrated daily utility:** retail, hawker centre, community club, bus
  interchange and green boulevard can support own-stay demand beyond a simple
  station-distance story.
- **CRL1 completion:** direct future access is a meaningful network upgrade if
  delivered on LTA's 2030 schedule.
- **Earlier possession than Pinery:** PARKTOWN's expected 30 June 2030 vacant
  possession is earlier than Pinery's published October 2031 possession
  notice, which can matter to owner-occupiers.
- **Proven launch depth:** 1,041 launch-weekend sales and later 94% booking
  demonstrate a broad initial buyer base.
- **Large-unit scarcity within the project:** only 47 4BR Premium and 22 5BR
  units exist, although scarcity does not remove the high-quantum constraint.
- **Tampines North maturation:** parks, new schools, public housing and town
  amenities should improve the lived environment over time.

## Risks

- **No owner exit proof:** the achieved ledger is developer New Sale evidence,
  not a realised owner premium.
- **Tail-selection risk:** remaining low-floor patio and high-quantum units may
  be the stock earlier buyers rejected on price or attributes.
- **Internal competition:** 1,193 homes create many comparable listings at
  completion even though only 69 are in the two largest types.
- **Future-transport risk:** CR6 is not operating, and the 2030 project and rail
  schedules can move independently.
- **Priced-in catalyst:** direct MRT and mall integration were core launch
  messages; a buyer cannot assume the whole eventual benefit remains
  unpriced.
- **Pinery competition:** Pinery establishes a higher price umbrella but offers
  direct access to an operating DTL station and a newer lease. Its premium
  cannot be copied mechanically.
- **Absolute-quantum liquidity:** the resale buyer must fund roughly
  S$3.6m–S$4.5m just for the seller to clear friction-only break-even.
- **Yield risk:** required rents for even 2.5%–3.0% gross yield are high, with
  no subject rental record yet.
- **Parking allocation:** 961 residential lots for 1,193 homes is about 0.81
  per unit. Large-family buyers should verify allocation and by-laws rather
  than assume a dedicated lot.
- **Mixed-use externalities:** convenience can come with crowding, vehicle
  circulation, loading activity, noise and management-interface issues.
- **Construction and valuation risk:** progressive financing, completion
  timing and bank valuation can weaken returns before resale.
- **Policy and tax risk:** ABSD, SSD, loan rules and property taxes are
  buyer-specific and can change before exit.

## Pre-booking due diligence

1. Obtain the developer-issued, timestamped availability and price list; do
   not rely on portal ad counts.
2. Match the exact unit to the approved floor plan, strata area and balcony or
   patio allocation. Price usable internal area separately from outdoor area.
3. Inspect the physical model and plans for road, bus interchange, retail
   loading, bin centre, mechanical equipment, pool, childcare, construction
   and future-site exposure.
4. Compare the chosen floor and stack against exact 2026 achieved PARKTOWN
   rows, not the project-wide median.
5. Obtain at least two bank valuations and loan illustrations that include
   progressive-payment interest and stress rates.
6. Confirm maintenance charges, parking rules, commercial/residential
   management boundaries and any use restrictions in the sale documents.
7. Verify the contractual vacant-possession date, completion date, defects
   process and delay remedies with a conveyancing lawyer.
8. Check Angsana Primary eligibility for the exact block through OneMap in the
   intended registration year.
9. Recheck CR6's opening status and the actual sheltered gate-to-platform
   route before attributing an operating-MRT premium.
10. Calculate BSD, ABSD, SSD, CPF refund, loan redemption and seller costs for
    the buyer's actual profile and intended exit date.

## Bottom line

PARKTOWN Residence has genuine product strength and exceptionally strong
take-up. The current opportunity is narrower: it is a choice among expensive
large homes where unit quality matters more than the project headline.

The **ordinary 1,496 sqft 4BR Premium near S$3.418m** is the cleanest current
benchmark. It sits below Pinery's matched large-unit median, near PARKTOWN's
own recent achieved band, and preserves a broader family-buyer exit pool than
the 5BR. A low S$3.322m patio unit can be better only if the patio and privacy
trade-offs are explicitly valued. The 5BR near S$3.858m–S$3.937m is an
own-stay lifestyle purchase; the S$4.151m end of the range should require an
exceptional stack and independent valuation.

On the stated capital-only model, **about S$0.1m gain after five years at 2%
annual price growth is plausible; roughly S$0.5m needs about 4% annual growth**
from the cheapest asks. After real financing and ownership costs, the net
gain will be lower. That is worthwhile potential for a long-hold owner, but
it is not evidence of a guaranteed or unusually large premium.
