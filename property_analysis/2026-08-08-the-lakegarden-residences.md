## Decision

For an unspecified 527 sqft unit, use **S$1.18 million to S$1.25 million** as the defensible 2026 exercised-price range and **S$1.22 million, or S$2,315 psf**, as the working base. The range is not equally applicable to every floor: S$1.18 million is the prudent low-floor or quick-sale case, S$1.20 million–S$1.22 million is the more supportable low/mid-floor zone, and S$1.23 million–S$1.25 million needs a better floor, view and patient buyer. Do not underwrite above S$1.25 million without an achieved subject-project secondary transaction or a demonstrably superior unit.

For the **median August 2023 purchase of S$1.0907 million**, that means:

- **gross price premium:** about **S$89,300–S$159,300**, or **8.2%–14.6%**;
- **base gross premium at S$1.22 million:** **S$129,300**, or **11.9%**;
- **capital-only profit after original BSD, a S$5,000 acquisition allowance and 2.5% exit friction:** about **S$26,600–S$94,800**, with **S$65,600 at the base**;
- **economic profit after mortgage interest, property tax, insurance and other holding costs:** lower, and possibly negative.

This is **not a huge-quantum exit**. Entry price matters as much as the 2026 market. At the S$1.22 million base, the lowest-priced August buyer has about **S$103,600** of capital-only profit, while the highest-priced buyer is approximately at breakeven before financing and holding costs.

There is still **no publicly captured achieved Sub Sale or Resale at The LakeGarden Residences** through the latest project data reviewed. The S$1.20 million–S$1.25 million evidence consists of asking campaigns, not completed transactions. URA also explains that secondary records depend on caveats, which are not mandatory, so “none captured” is strong but not conclusive evidence that no private deal exists.

## Capital-only profit after transaction costs

The capital model deliberately states its convention:

- Singapore citizen buying a first residential property in August 2023;
- no ABSD, or all ABSD separately remitted/refunded;
- acquisition basis equals original price plus 2023 BSD plus a S$5,000 legal/technical allowance;
- exit proceeds equal 97.5% of sale price, using a 2.5% planning allowance for agency, GST, sale legal and possible assignment friction;
- SSD is zero only if the legally effective disposal occurs on or after the applicable third anniversary;
- mortgage/progressive-payment interest, property tax, insurance, maintenance, opportunity cost and rent are excluded.

[IRAS's BSD schedule](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29) applies 1% to the first S$180,000, 2% to the next S$180,000, 3% to the next S$640,000 and 4% to the next S$500,000 for these 2023 prices. The 2.5% exit allowance is not a statutory fee: [CEA says property-agent commissions are not fixed and GST treatment must be agreed](https://www.cea.gov.sg/consumers/engaging-a-property-agent/what-to-take-note-of-when-engaging-a-property-agent/).

Formula:

`capital-only profit = 0.975 × sale price − purchase price − original BSD − S$5,000`

## SSD timing can remove S$47,000–S$50,000

The [IRAS SSD rules](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property) preserve the three-year schedule for homes acquired from 11 March 2017 through 3 July 2025: 4% applies when disposal occurs more than two but within three years, and no SSD applies after three years. The 2025 change to a four-year period applies only to acquisitions from 4 July 2025.

IRAS generally uses the buyer's acceptance of the seller's Option to Purchase, the Sale & Purchase Agreement, or the transfer date where the earlier instruments do not apply. Completion date is not the default SSD date. For a public purchase record dated 4, 5 or 8 August 2023, the corresponding third anniversary would be 4, 5 or 8 August 2026 **only if that public date is the legally controlling acquisition date**.

The stamped documents and solicitor's opinion must control. If a buyer legally accepted one day too early, 4% SSD would be:

| Sale price | 4% SSD | Median-buyer capital profit after that SSD |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| S$1.18m | S$47,200 | **-S$20,628** |
| S$1.22m | S$48,800 | **+S$16,772** |
| S$1.25m | S$50,000 | **+S$44,822** |

At this report's 8 August 2026 capture time, all three public anniversaries have arrived. That does not replace legal verification because a developer OTP can be subject to signing the Sale & Purchase Agreement, changing the operative acquisition date.

## ABSD, financing and cash proceeds

This report's profit table assumes no ABSD. Under [IRAS's rates effective from 27 April 2023](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29), a Singapore citizen's first home attracted no ABSD, a Permanent Resident's first home attracted 5%, and a citizen's second home attracted 20%, subject to any applicable remission.

For the median S$1.0907 million purchase:

- unrecovered **5% ABSD** is about S$54,535 and reduces the S$65,572 base profit to about **S$11,037**;
- unrecovered **20% ABSD** is about S$218,140 and changes the base result to about a **S$152,568 loss**.

Every S$10,000 of mortgage interest or other unrecovered holding cost reduces economic profit by S$10,000. Conversely, repaying loan principal is not an economic loss even though it reduces the cash cheque.

Cash released at completion is a different calculation:

`cash released = sale price − sale costs − SSD − bank redemption − CPF refund − completion adjustments`

CPF principal plus accrued interest normally returns to the owner's CPF account rather than disappearing; the [CPF Board explains the sale-refund mechanics](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/home-ownership/using-your-cpf-to-buy-a-home/cpf-refund-when-selling-or-transferring-property). Without the bank redemption statement, CPF usage and accrued-interest figure, this report cannot state the seller's cash proceeds.

## 2026 is likely a pre-completion sale

The official brochure states a 99-year lease from 31 May 2023 and **expected vacant possession on 29 August 2027**. Unless possession occurs early, a 2026 disposal is an uncompleted-property sale or assignment. The owner therefore needs a conveyancing solicitor to verify the original Sale & Purchase Agreement, developer notification, outstanding progressive payments, bank and CPF redemption, assignment mechanics, defect/warranty rights and whether title has issued.

CEA publishes a dedicated [seller checklist for uncompleted private residential property](https://www.cea.gov.sg/docs/default-source/Professionals/Agreements-Checklists/checklist-for-seller-%28sale-of-uncompleted-private-residential-property%29_jan21.pdf). The extra legal and financing work can narrow the buyer pool relative to completed J Gateway, Lake Grande and Lakefront homes that can be occupied or rented immediately.

## Seller action thresholds

1. **Confirm the exact unit and entry:** obtain the stamped contract, purchase price, BSD/ABSD certificate and the legally controlling acquisition date.
2. **Clear the sale structure before marketing:** have a solicitor confirm zero SSD and the uncompleted-property transfer process in writing.
3. **Price against evidence, not the desired profit:** use S$1.18m as the low/quick-sale reference, S$1.22m as the generic base, and S$1.25m only for a stronger floor/view case.
4. **Demand fresh proof from agents:** ask for the exact unit, CEA-verified mandate, listing start date, written offers, failed valuation feedback and any exercised 527 sqft transaction—not portal card counts.
5. **Compute actual economics:** subtract unrecovered ABSD, total loan interest, sale legal/agency costs and holding costs from the capital-only table; separately calculate bank/CPF cash release.
6. **Reprice when a real subject comp appears:** the first achieved LakeGarden 527 sqft secondary transaction should carry more weight than every current asking campaign.
