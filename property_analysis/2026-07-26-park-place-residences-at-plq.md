## Decision

Park Place Residences at PLQ is a strong, defensive integrated development,
but the evidence does not support underwriting **huge capital appreciation** at
most advertised prices. Direct integration with Paya Lebar MRT, offices and
retail supports rental demand, resale liquidity and a persistent premium. Much
of that premium is already visible in achieved prices.

The best apparent opportunity in the captured inventory is the advertised
**667 sqft two-bedroom/two-bathroom unit at S$1.50m**, or approximately
S$2,249 psf. If S$1.50m is a genuine executable seller price rather than an
offer teaser, it is below recent same-size caveats of S$1.57m, S$1.58m and
S$1.61m. The listing is labelled "Starting From" and has been syndicated by
multiple agents, so the price, exact unit and availability must be verified
before treating the discount as real.

Evidence-backed buy boxes are:

1. **Best apparent mispricing:** 667 sqft 2BR/2BA at S$1.50m, subject to
   seller-price verification.
2. **Best repeatable balanced purchase:** 646 sqft 2BR/1BA at no more than
   approximately S$1.48m.
3. **Best income-oriented purchase:** 484 sqft 1BR at no more than
   approximately S$1.10m.
4. **Best family/own-stay value:** 1,163 sqft 3BR+study around
   S$2.65m–S$2.70m.
5. **Poor appreciation entry points:** 667 sqft above S$1.64m, 1,076 sqft
   above S$2.60m and 1,163 sqft close to S$3.00m.

Here, "quantum potential" means whether the present total purchase price leaves
credible resale and capital-appreciation headroom. If quantum means only the
absolute entry ticket, the S$1.10m–S$1.50m one- and two-bedroom units have a
comparatively liquid buyer pool. Low absolute quantum should not be confused
with undervaluation because compact units can still carry high PSF and face
competition from many similar layouts.

## Scope and evidence rules

This report separates three evidence classes:

- **Advertised asking inventory:** evidence that an agent is marketing a unit,
  not proof that the unit remains unsold or that the seller will accept the
  headline price.
- **Achieved caveats:** stronger price evidence based on transactions lodged
  with URA, but without complete unit-level attributes such as renovation,
  facing and exact internal condition.
- **Scenario underwriting:** explicit analytical assumptions, not a price
  forecast or guaranteed return.

Primary achieved-price source:
[URA Property Market Information](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/property-market-information/pmiResidentialTransactionSearch).
URA explains that resale and subsale records are based on caveats lodged after
an option or sale-and-purchase agreement and are updated twice weekly; caveats
are voluntary and not exhaustive. See
[URA REALIS coverage and methodology](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/reis/coverageandMethodology).

The convenient project transaction table used to reconcile exact floor areas
is the
[99.co Park Place project record](https://www.99.co/singapore/condos-apartments/park-place-residences-at-plq).

## Five-year capital-return test

The following illustration tests the strongest advertised candidate: a
S$1.50m purchase by a Singapore citizen buying a first residential property.

Assumptions:

- buyer's stamp duty: S$44,600;
- purchase legal cost: S$3,000;
- sale legal cost: S$3,000;
- selling commission: 2% plus 9% GST, or 2.18% of exit price;
- no ABSD;
- no mortgage interest, maintenance, property tax, vacancy or rental income;
  and
- sale after the SSD holding period.

Current duty references:
[IRAS buyer's stamp duty](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29),
[IRAS ABSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29)
and
[IRAS SSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property).

| Five-year price growth | Exit price | Approx. capital-only profit/loss |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0% p.a. | S$1.500m | -S$83k |
| 2% p.a. | S$1.656m | +S$69k |
| 3% p.a. | S$1.739m | +S$150k |
| 4% p.a. | S$1.825m | +S$235k |
| 5% p.a. | S$1.914m | +S$322k |

The approximate capital-only break-even exit is **S$1.585m**, 5.7% above the
purchase price. Producing approximately S$300,000 of capital profit requires
an exit close to S$1.91m, or about **S$2,869 psf** for 667 sqft. That is about
17% above the project's existing PSF record, so a "huge" gain requires a bull
case rather than the base case.

For a Singapore citizen buying a second residential property, a 20% ABSD adds
S$300,000 at entry. Under the same simplified assumptions, capital break-even
rises to approximately **S$1.892m**, or 26.1% above the purchase price. This is
approximately 4.75% annual appreciation for five years merely to recover
transaction costs before holding costs.

Purchases made on or after 4 July 2025 must be held for more than four years to
avoid SSD under the current rules. Buyer nationality, residency, property
count and ownership structure must be checked against current IRAS rules
before applying the example.

## Risks and limits to upside

- **The integration premium is already capitalised.** Current project prices
  are above nearby non-integrated stock and close to newer Parc Esta.
- **Most old catalysts are realised.** PLQ, the mall, offices and MRT
  integration already exist. CCL6 is now open rather than a future surprise.
- **Compact-unit competition:** the original project release indicates that
  roughly 82% of units are one- or two-bedroom layouts. This supports a broad
  rental and buyer pool but gives future purchasers many close substitutes.
  [Original Lendlease project release](https://www.lendlease.com/contentassets/9f45bc468d434e518b51a07ead6f8565/documents/20170309-park-residences-plq.pdf).
- **Leasehold differentiation:** the project has a 99-year lease beginning in
  2015, leaving approximately 88 years at the report date. There is no acute
  five-to-ten-year lease issue, but it should not be valued as freehold.
- **Supply and market cycle:** URA reported overall private prices up 0.5% in
  Q2 2026 but RCR non-landed prices down 1.2%, RCR rents flat and a substantial
  private-housing pipeline.
  [URA Q2 2026 statistics](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr26-57/).
- **Long-horizon development is double-edged:** the former air-base district
  may improve amenity and employment access, but it will also introduce a large
  amount of future housing supply and has a remote delivery horizon.
- **Unit-specific losses can dominate project quality:** road/MRT/service-bay
  noise, west sun, low-floor privacy, view obstruction, tenancy terms,
  maintenance liabilities and renovation quality are not visible in
  project-level PSF.

## Due diligence before an option to purchase

For the S$1.50m 667 sqft candidate, obtain:

1. The exact block, unit number, floor, stack and written seller-authorised
   reserve or executable price. Do not rely on "Starting From."
2. Proof that the unit remains available and clarification of conflicting
   vacant, owner-occupied or tenanted descriptions across syndicated adverts.
3. The seller's acquisition date, title/caveat record and any SSD constraint.
4. A bank valuation and exact stack/floor caveats rather than project-wide
   averages alone.
5. A daytime and evening inspection for MRT, road, mall loading/service noise,
   west sun, privacy and potential view blockage.
6. MCST maintenance and sinking-fund amounts, AGM minutes, special levies,
   defects, water ingress and renovation approvals.
7. If tenanted, the complete tenancy agreement, deposit transfer, rent,
   expiry, break clauses, tenant history and inventory list.
8. Buyer-specific ABSD, financing, total debt servicing ratio, cash-flow
   buffer and intended exit horizon.

## Final assessment

Park Place Residences has **high defensive quality and moderate appreciation
potential**, not high-confidence huge upside. Its strongest investment case is
integrated convenience plus rental resilience at a disciplined entry price.

The S$1.50m 667 sqft advertisement could provide approximately S$70k–S$110k of
immediate price headroom against recent same-size caveats, but only if the
advertised figure is genuine and the unit does not have a material
floor/stack/condition disadvantage. At prevailing high asks, buyers are paying
for the project's quality in advance and should expect ordinary market-like
compounding rather than an exceptional quantum windfall.
