## Decision

144A Lorong Sarina has **moderate freehold land-bank and own-stay potential**,
but the available evidence does not support high-confidence **huge
risk-adjusted investment quantum** at an ordinary fair-market entry. Three
more-recent mechanical market reads cluster around S$4.88m–S$5.12m for broadly
comparable condition, while the much older 2010 sale produces a sharply lower
index diagnostic. At S$4.8m, even an eight-year 3% annual sale-price gain
produces only about S$886,000 of capital-only profit before financing and
holding costs.

No publicly indexed live or archived asking advertisement could be verified as
the exact house as of the research timestamp. The current S$5.8m–S$5.9m Lorong
Sarina advertisements describe materially larger, approximately 3,500 sqft
corner-terrace plots and are excluded. There is therefore no honest
address-specific “best listing” or executable asking price to recommend.

The evidence-backed conditional entry ladder is:

1. **At or below S$4.60m, or about S$1,869 land psf:** potentially compelling
   for a Singapore-citizen first-property buyer if title, net frontage,
   drainage, party-wall, approved-plan and structural checks are clean.
2. **S$4.60m–S$4.85m:** defensible for an ordinary liveable house, with enough
   headroom for moderate works but not an uncontrolled rebuild.
3. **S$4.85m–S$5.10m:** broadly fair-market territory. Buy for verified
   condition, layout and own-stay utility rather than a presumed valuation
   error.
4. **Above S$5.10m:** requires excellent existing build-up, renovation,
   frontage, privacy or a professionally verified redevelopment envelope.
   Freehold tenure alone does not justify the premium.

A speculative full rebuild is not an obvious quantum strategy. A S$4.60m
purchase plus current buyer's stamp duty, S$15,000 of acquisition
legal/technical costs and Haio's S$1.20m–S$1.38m **structure-only** model already
creates an incomplete S$6.03m–S$6.21m basis. Break-even would require about
S$6.19m–S$6.37m before accounting for demolition, consultants, approvals,
drainage, contingency, finance and temporary housing.

Here, “huge quantum” means credible room for a large absolute-dollar profit
after entry and exit friction, not merely that a landed house has a large
headline price. A S$2m capital-only profit from a S$4.8m as-is purchase requires
an exit near S$7.22m, or S$2,935 land psf. That is possible only as a bull
scenario, not supported as a base-case forecast.

## Planning and redevelopment potential

URA's
[current prevailing landed controls](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/property-and-business-owners/property/renovating-private-residential-property/land-titled-residential/prevailing-planning-controls/)
set generic minimums for a side-to-side semi-detached or Type I corner terrace
of:

- 200 sqm net plot area;
- 8 m net plot width;
- generally 7.5 m front setback for category 3–5 roads;
- 2 m side and rear setbacks; and
- 1 m side/rear roof-eave clearance.

URA's
[semi-detached plot-size guidance](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/development-control/development-control-handbooks/residential/semi-detached-houses/plot-size-width/)
expressly says plot area and width are net of land required for State vesting,
including drainage or road reserves. The gross 228.6 sqm arithmetic therefore
does not prove redevelopment compliance. A cadastral/topographical survey,
Road Line Plan and drainage-reserve check are essential.

[URA's semi-detached handbook](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/development-control/development-control-handbooks/residential/semi-detached-houses/)
also says landed permissible GFA results from allowable building height and
envelope, not a generic plot-ratio multiplication.

A coordinate point screen against the public
[Master Plan 2025 SDCP Landed Housing Area layer](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_70a5a4b67d9171dc0db6f6fd259a3215/view)
did **not** place the exact OneMap point inside a displayed Landed Housing Area
polygon. That indicative screen is not a Certified Interpretation, does not
establish the site's land-use zoning or development rights, and does not mean
that non-landed redevelopment is permitted. The exact parcel boundary,
street-block controls, two- or three-storey envelope, absolute technical
height, platform level and special controls still require verification through
[URA SPACE's control-plan service](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/maps/index.html?service=CTRLPLAN)
and a Qualified Person. The base case assigns no premium to either presumed
regulatory landed safeguarding or presumed upzoning.

For works that retain the house,
[URA's A&A criteria](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/development-control/development-control-handbooks/residential/semi-detached-houses/additions-alterations/)
generally limit proposed additional GFA, removed/replaced external walls and
structural changes to 50%. Works outside the criteria are treated as
reconstruction. An A&A route may preserve value and reduce cost, but only a
structural engineer and architect can determine whether the existing house
makes it viable.

- buyer's stamp duty: S$227,600;
- purchase legal, survey and initial technical due diligence: S$15,000;

For a residential price above S$3m, current BSD is S$119,600 plus 6% of the
amount above S$3m. Duty applies to the higher of consideration or market value.

[IRAS BSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29),

and
[IRAS SSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property).

The day-one basis is approximately **S$5.043m** before financing, works and

## ABSD and eligibility test

At a S$4.80m entry, a Singapore citizen buying a second residential property
without remission pays 20% ABSD, or **S$960,000**. The simplified basis rises
to approximately **S$6.003m** before carry.

| Second-property target | Required gross exit | Exit land rate | Required 5-year sale-price CAGR | Required 8-year sale-price CAGR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Capital-only break-even | S$6.157m | S$2,502 psf | 5.10% | 3.16% |
| S$1m capital-only profit | S$7.182m | S$2,918 psf | 8.39% | 5.17% |
| S$2m capital-only profit | S$8.208m | S$3,335 psf | 11.33% | 6.94% |

This makes 144A a poor pure-investment candidate for most second-property
buyers at fair value. Remissions and buyer profiles are fact-specific; do not
assume one applies.

Purchases made on or after 4 July 2025 generally attract SSD of 16%, 12%, 8% or
4% if sold within the first, second, third or fourth year respectively, and no
SSD only after the precise holding period is exceeded under current rules.

Landed eligibility is also narrower than condominium eligibility.
[SLA's foreign-ownership guidance](https://www.sla.gov.sg/regulatory/foreign-ownership-of-property/)
says a foreign person, including a Singapore permanent resident for this
purpose, requires approval to acquire a semi-detached house. Eligibility,
ownership structure and approval must be resolved before an OTP is exercised.

S$1.20m–S$1.38m based on a modelled 3,446–3,447 sqft build-up. That output is

At a S$4.60m acquisition:

- BSD is approximately S$215,600;
- assumed acquisition legal/technical cost is S$15,000; and
- acquisition basis before works is approximately S$4.831m.

Adding only the structure model gives:

| Structure-only model | Incomplete project basis | Break-even gross exit after 2.5% sale friction | Break-even land rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| S$1.20m | S$6.031m | S$6.185m | S$2,513 psf |

cost may be materially higher.

An illustrative **S$6.50m total project basis** is therefore only a placeholder,
not a budget. It leaves just S$289,400–S$469,400 above acquisition plus the
structure-only model for every omitted item.

| Target on a S$6.50m first-property project basis | Required gross exit | Exit land rate |
|---|---:|---:|

| S$1m capital-only profit | S$7.692m | S$3,126 psf |

The placeholder break-even rate is already close to the April 2026
S$2,851-psf **new-sale** Kembangan semi-detached comp. Once the omitted items,

unless land is bought deeply below S$4.60m, the existing structure supports a

A buyer who must pay second-property ABSD adds another S$920,000 at a S$4.60m

underwrite; this is primarily an own-stay/freehold-land proposition, not a
yield-led investment.

## Location and liveability

The exact OneMap coordinates are approximately:

- **513 m straight-line from Kembangan MRT**; and
- **579 m straight-line from Eunos MRT**.

These are coordinate calculations, not walking routes. Actual gate-to-station
distance, shelter, crossings and walking time must be checked on foot. Being
between two East West Line stations provides useful transport redundancy but
is not the same as direct MRT integration.

The
[December 2025 Stacked walk-through](https://stackedhomes.com/lorong-sarina-landed-enclave-review/)
records practical nearby amenities along Changi Road, including eateries,
provision shops, clinics and bus services. It also records several
micro-location trade-offs:

- limited street parking on much of the enclave;
- branch streets with centre white lines;
- rows separated by a canal or drain;
- variable privacy and road exposure; and
- Changi Road and Sims Avenue East traffic that can sound different by facing
  and peak period.

These are estate-level observations, not a 144A inspection. Visit weekday
morning peak, school-run time, evening peak, late evening and after heavy rain.

No source in the sweep reliably established a named primary school within 1 km
for this exact postal address. Haio's model places its closest listed primary
school just beyond 1 km, but home-school distance is an address-specific
admissions matter. Verify the exact address in
[MOE SchoolFinder](https://www.moe.gov.sg/schoolfinder) and do not pay a school
premium based on a generic project pin.

## Flood and drainage due diligence

On 12 December 2025, PUB reported a flash flood on Sims Avenue East from Lorong
Sarina to Lorong Mydin at about 4.25 pm; it had subsided by 5 pm:
[CNA's contemporaneous report relaying PUB's update](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/flash-floods-heavy-rain-upper-east-coast-road-pub-5578451).

This proves a recent access-road event in the immediate locality. It does
**not** establish that floodwater entered 144A, reached its gate, or affected
the structure. PUB's
[November 2025 published flood-prone-area list](https://www.pub.gov.sg/-/media/PUB/PDF/Flood-Management/List-of-Flood-Prone-Areas-as-at-Nov-2025.pdf)
did not name Lorong Sarina or this Sims Avenue East stretch, but the December
event post-dates that list. PUB's
[February 2026 hotspot list](https://www.pub.gov.sg/-/media/PUB/PDF/Flood-Management/List-of-Hotspots-in-Singapore_Feb-2026.pdf)
also does not name the street. These lists do not replace site-level flood
history, levels or drainage checks.

Before purchase or rebuild, obtain:

- seller disclosure and insurance/repair records for water entry;
- exact road, gate, car-porch and internal finished-floor levels;
- PUB minimum platform level and drainage requirements;
- drainage-reserve and canal setback information;
- stormwater discharge and anti-backflow design; and
- an inspection during or immediately after intense rain if possible.

Flood resilience can materially affect rebuild cost and usable ground-floor
design. It should not be reduced to a binary portal risk label.

## Catalysts and optionality

- **Kembangan station-area renewal:** URA's
  [East Unleashed plan](https://www.ura.gov.sg/land-planning/master-plan/master-plan-2025/recreation-master-plan/east-unleashed-recreation-opportunities-for-everyone/)
  says a redeveloped Kembangan Community Club and new park will be built with
  the public-housing project next to Kembangan Station, potentially adding a
  community plaza, shops, gym and futsal court. This is a practical amenity
  improvement, but much of the story is public and should not be capitalised as
  a windfall.
- **Paya Lebar Air Base relocation:** URA's
  [Master Plan 2025 PLAB page](https://www.ura.gov.sg/land-planning/master-plan/master-plan-2025/regional-plans/east/paya-lebar-air-base/)
  says relocation will begin from the 2030s, followed by phased new homes,
  workspaces, transport, green and blue networks.
- **Height-restriction relief:** URA's
  [long-term planning release](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr22-25/)
  says PLAB relocation will free about 800 ha and allow surrounding height
  restrictions to be lifted. This may improve wider-area redevelopment and
  demand over a long horizon; it does not automatically upzone 144A or override
  any applicable site-specific controls.
- **Current landed-market momentum:** URA reported landed prices up 2.5%
  quarter on quarter in 2Q 2026. One strong quarter and a national index are
  not a Kembangan forecast. URA simultaneously advises purchase prudence amid
  an uncertain macroeconomic outlook.

The first two catalysts improve long-run liveability and the wider demand
catchment. PLAB is especially long-dated and will also introduce substantial
new housing supply. Neither catalyst is booked into the base return scenarios.

## Risks and limits to upside

- **No executable subject ask:** a valuation range cannot reveal whether an
  off-market seller would transact within it.
- **Unknown existing asset:** actual approved GFA, age, layout, defects,
  additions and liveability are the largest missing value variables.
- **Very thin direct evidence:** the latest exact-address sale is from 2019 and
  the strongest adjacent comp is from 2022.
- **Subtype and title ambiguity:** public portals conflict on “terrace” versus
  “semi-detached”; legal title and approved housing form are decisive.
- **Small development-control margin:** displayed gross area is only about
  28.6 sqm above the generic semi-detached minimum before reserve checks.
- **Party-wall and neighbour dependency:** 144B's structure, foundations,
  approvals and construction plans can affect design and execution.
- **Flood/drainage exposure:** a recent nearby road flash flood makes levels,
  drainage and insurance history material due diligence.
- **Road and canal micro-location:** noise, privacy, mosquitoes, odour and
  water-management experience can vary by exact row and facing.
- **Parking constraints:** the estate walk-through documents scarce street
  parking on several branches; exact driveway capacity matters to family
  utility.
- **No verified 1 km primary-school premium:** address-specific MOE evidence is
  required.
- **Rebuild execution risk:** hard-structure estimates omit many large,
  site-specific costs and schedule risk.
- **High-quantum liquidity:** the future buyer pool is narrower than for a
  compact condominium, and exact same-type transactions are infrequent.
- **Tax and eligibility:** ABSD can erase ordinary upside, SSD penalises a short
  hold, and foreign persons require landed-property approval.
- **Freehold is not maintenance-free:** tenure protects against lease decay but
  does not prevent physical obsolescence or guarantee redevelopment approval.

## Due diligence before an option to purchase

Obtain and verify:

1. Seller-authorised exact asking price, proof of authority to market, same-day
   availability and seller timeline.
2. SLA title, ownership, tenure, encumbrances, caveats, lot number, legal
   housing form and 144A/144B party-wall/pair status.
3. Registered survey, cadastral/topographical survey, exact net site area,
   average/narrowest frontage, plot shape and boundary occupation.
4. Road Line Plan, drainage reserve, canal/back-lane status, access approval,
   servitudes and utility/sewer alignments.
5. Approved URA/BCA plans, existing approved GFA, storeys, attic/basement,
   additions, planning permissions, structural records, TOP/CSC and
   unauthorised-work risk.
6. A Qualified Person's site-specific planning study covering landed
   designation, storey/height envelope, setbacks, platform level, party-wall
   treatment and an A&A-versus-reconstruction comparison.
7. Structural engineer and building surveyor inspection for foundations,
   settlement, roof, waterproofing, termites, drainage, electrical/plumbing,
   asbestos risk and retained-structure viability.
8. Quantity-surveyor cost plan with demolition, consultants, approvals, site
   works, neighbour protection, contingency, finance and temporary housing,
   followed by comparable tenders.
9. Day, peak-hour, evening and heavy-rain inspections for rail/road noise,
   parking, access, privacy, sun, canal conditions and water levels.
10. Fresh URA/REALIS caveats restricted to similar-size freehold
    semi-detached houses and split by new sale versus resale; obtain a bank and
    professional valuation.
11. Buyer-specific LDAU eligibility, BSD/ABSD/SSD treatment, financing/TDSR,
    cash/CPF requirement, interest-rate stress and a long-hold cash-flow
    buffer.
12. If rent matters, exact landed lease comparables, tenant profile, achievable
    rent, non-owner-occupier tax, vacancy, repair and leasing-cost assumptions.

Do not exercise an OTP on the strength of a portal land polygon, generic street
listing or contractor headline alone.

## Final assessment

144A Lorong Sarina is a potentially attractive **freehold own-stay acquisition
with moderate appreciation potential**, especially if it can be acquired at or
below approximately S$4.60m with a usable, legally approved structure. Two MRT
stations, East-side amenities and long-term Kembangan/PLAB renewal support a
patient holding case.

At the S$4.88m–S$5.12m mechanically indicated ordinary-condition range, the
buyer is paying broadly evidenced market value rather than capturing a huge
mispricing. A S$1m capital-only profit from S$4.8m requires an exit around
S$6.20m; a S$2m result requires about S$7.22m. Carrying and works costs make the
real thresholds higher.

The speculative-rebuild case is weaker. Even an incomplete structure-only
budget requires a S$2,513–S$2,588 psf break-even exit from a S$4.60m land
entry. A S$6.50m total-basis placeholder moves break-even to about S$2,709 psf,
close to the latest local new-sale evidence before full execution risk is
priced.

The practical strategy is to buy the exact title and physical house below the
condition-matched range, preserve a viable A&A option if possible, and hold
beyond SSD. Do not pay for an unverified condominium-assembly story, automatic
PLAB upzoning, modelled build-up or hidden house number. At fair-market entry,
expect market-like landed compounding and own-stay utility—not a
high-confidence huge quantum windfall.
