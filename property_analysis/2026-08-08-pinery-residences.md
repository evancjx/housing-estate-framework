Summary: **Pinery is a strong long-hold own-stay proposition, but not an asymmetric investment at roughly S$2,550 psf: most compact homes have been taken up, the publicly indicated balance is concentrated in S$2.97m–S$3.90m four- and five-bedroom stock, and a current buyer generally needs a long hold or sustained 4% annual price growth to approach a large five-year capital-only gain before financing and holding costs.**

## Decision

Pinery Residences has genuine scarcity value: the developer confirms a **planned direct underground pedestrian connection to Tampines West MRT**, a full commercial mall below the homes and a 2025-starting lease. It also demonstrated exceptional primary-market demand. Those strengths make it a credible long-hold owner-occupier purchase.

They do not make the launch price cheap. The project sold 544 of 588 homes over its launch weekend at an average **S$2,546 psf**. The captured URA-derived transaction sample averages about S$2,550 psf, while nearby completed private homes of matching sizes generally transact around S$1,450–S$1,950 psf and the larger ParkTown Residence new-launch control averages mostly around S$2,300–S$2,440 psf by size. Pinery buyers are paying for the newer lease, future mall and direct rail link upfront.

For a buyer entering on 8 August 2026, the decision is:

- **Own stay for at least eight years:** reasonable if the exact stack, floor and layout work, the household values weather-proof rail and daily retail, and the mortgage remains comfortable under a rate stress test.
- **Capital-gain investment:** selective, not compelling. Do not buy merely because the project is “integrated.” At the publicly indicated balance prices, prefer lower-PSF large formats only after verifying why they remain and whether a ground/PES, road, mall-service, public-plaza or facilities exposure explains the discount.
- **Rental investment:** weakly evidenced. There can be no achieved Pinery rent before completion, and present-day surrounding rent bands imply only roughly 2.0%–2.8% headline gross yields against Pinery entry prices before any completion-date or unit adjustment.
- **Short trade or pre-TOP flip:** unattractive. A 2026 acquisition is subject to the four-year Seller's Stamp Duty schedule, and the brochure's contractual notice-of-vacant-possession date is October 2031.

The most defensible publicly indicated balance starting points were approximately **S$2.972 million for 1,195 sqft**, **S$3.124 million–S$3.172 million for 1,227 sqft**, **S$3.375 million for 1,389 sqft**, and **S$3.579 million for 1,475 sqft**. These are appointed-agent advertised starting prices, not same-day executable developer quotations. Do not pay a high-floor or preferred-stack premium without comparing the exact dollar gap against the same type's achieved caveats.

This is therefore a **quality-home / fully-priced-investment** conclusion. A “huge” five-year gain is possible only under a strong market path or a materially discounted entry; it is not the base case supported by today's evidence.

## Scope and evidence rules

This report keeps four evidence classes separate:

1. **Official project facts** come from the developer, government tender record and project brochure.
2. **Achieved New Sales** are developer sales or URA-derived transaction records. They are not resales and do not prove future owner liquidity.
3. **Advertised balance and portal cards** are leads only. They can lag, duplicate one unit, preserve a sold unit's launch price or misclassify its bedroom count.
4. **Upside and rental scenarios** are transparent calculations, not forecasts, valuations or promises.

The official starting point for achieved private-home data is [URA Property Market Information](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/property-market-information/pmiResidentialTransactionSearch). Public project interfaces identify their transaction source as URA, but caveat timing, removed options and portal refresh dates can make counts differ. A developer “sold” count, a portal transaction count and an appointed-agent balance chart are not interchangeable.

## The MRT connection is real, but future

Hoi Hup states that the completed project will have **direct underground pedestrian access to Tampines West MRT Station on the Downtown Line**. The developer-side [Sunway preview](https://sunwayproperty.com/news-events/hoi-hup-sunway-mcl-preview-pinery-residences-mixed-use-project-tampines-west-2340-psf/) describes a 24-hour public through-link across Pinery Mall to the underground MRT link.

That supports “direct MRT connection” as a completed-project attribute. It should not be written as though residents can use it on 8 August 2026: the development is under construction. The public route also means the mall and link will serve the wider neighbourhood, not only residents. Separate residential access is a positive design response, but a buyer should still inspect:

- the stack's distance from residential lift lobbies and the MRT through-link;
- public-plaza, food-court, loading-bay, bin-centre and service-road exposure;
- after-hours access and security separation;
- whether the route remains fully sheltered at every transition; and
- the approved construction drawings rather than a brochure diagram.

Tampines West is one Downtown Line stop from Tampines interchange. Direct rail is a durable demand feature, but the travel-time benefit was known at launch and is already reflected in the S$2,500-plus psf achieved pricing.

## Five- and eight-year quantum scenarios

The scenario isolates capital movement. It uses three entry references:

- **S$1.683m:** captured median for the sold-out two-bedroom family, useful for an original-buyer reference but not a current developer offer;
- **S$3.172m:** appointed-agent starting indication for a 1,227 sqft D3 conventional unit; and
- **S$3.579m:** appointed-agent starting indication for a 1,475 sqft E1-G unit.

Assumptions:

- Singapore citizen buying a first residential property, so no ABSD;
- BSD under the current residential brackets;
- S$5,000 purchase legal/valuation allowance;
- exit after the applicable four-year SSD period;
- all-in selling friction of 2.5%, so net sale proceeds equal 97.5% of gross price;
- gross price compounds at 2%, 3% or 4% annually purely for illustration; and
- no mortgage interest, property tax, maintenance, insurance, renovation, opportunity cost, rent or vacancy.

The five-year horizon falls in August 2031, before the brochure's contractual 9 October 2031 vacant-possession date. Unless lawful possession occurs earlier, that exit is an uncompleted-property subsale or assignment; the 2.5% friction allowance may omit additional assignment, financing and legal costs.

`capital-only profit = 0.975 × scenario exit price − entry price − BSD − S$5,000`

| Entry reference | BSD | 5 years at 2% / 3% / 4% | 8 years at 2% / 3% / 4% |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **S$1.683m compact reference** | S$53,750 | **S$70k / S$161k / S$255k** | **S$181k / S$337k / S$504k** |
| **S$3.172m 4BR reference** | S$129,920 | **S$108k / S$278k / S$456k** | **S$317k / S$611k / S$926k** |
| **S$3.579m 5BR reference** | S$154,340 | **S$114k / S$307k / S$507k** | **S$350k / S$682k / S$1.037m** |

At 3% annual growth, gross five-year prices would be about S$1.951 million, S$3.677 million and S$4.149 million respectively. The large units generate more absolute dollars because more capital is exposed; they do not generate a better percentage return.

The table explains the decision:

- a **S$500,000 capital-only gain in five years** requires roughly the 4% path even for the 5BR and is not reached by the S$3.172 million 4BR reference;
- at an eight-year hold, the 3% path crosses S$500,000 for the large units, but financing and holding costs can materially reduce it; and
- the apparent transaction-cost breakeven growth rate is only about 1.2%–1.4% annually over five years, but this excludes the largest real cost for a leveraged buyer: interest.

Every S$100,000 of cumulative interest and unrecovered holding cost reduces these profits by S$100,000. A second-property Singapore-citizen buyer's 20% ABSD would be S$336,600, S$634,400 or S$715,800 on the three entry references, before BSD. That overwhelms most five-year capital-only scenarios unless a remission applies.

## SSD, progressive payments and exit timing

[IRAS's current SSD schedule](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property) applies to Pinery buyers because the acquisition occurs after 4 July 2025:

- up to one year: 16%;
- more than one and up to two years: 12%;
- more than two and up to three years: 8%;
- more than three and up to four years: 4%; and
- more than four years: no SSD.

IRAS generally measures from the legally effective acquisition and disposal instruments, not the portal transaction month or key-collection date. Obtain a solicitor's written date calculation before marketing.

The four-year anniversary of a March 2026 purchase falls around March 2030, before the brochure's October 2031 vacant-possession notice date. A sale in that window would still be a sale/assignment of uncompleted property. CEA publishes dedicated [uncompleted-private-property buyer and seller checklists](https://www.cea.gov.sg/consumers/transacting-on-your-own/buying-or-selling-a-private-residential-property/). The solicitor must check developer notification, assignment documents, title status, outstanding progress payments, bank/CPF redemption and any unit-specific restrictions.

[URA's Home Buyer's Guide](https://www.ura.gov.sg/-/media/Corporate/Property/Home-Buyers-Guide/Home-Buyers-Guide-Sep-2025-full.pdf) explains that an uncompleted-property buyer pays under the contracted progress-payment process. Cash and interest exposure therefore rise as construction progresses. The exact drawdown path, loan rate and CPF use are required for an economic-profit model.

## Other purchase costs and buyer-profile risks

[IRAS's BSD brackets](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29) apply on the higher of price or market value: 1% on the first S$180,000, 2% on the next S$180,000, 3% on the next S$640,000, 4% on the next S$500,000, 5% on the next S$1.5 million and 6% above S$3 million.

[IRAS's current ABSD table](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29) makes buyer profile decisive. At capture, a Singapore citizen's first home has no ABSD, second home 20% and third/subsequent home 30%; a Permanent Resident's first home is 5%; and a foreigner's purchase is generally 60%, subject to treaty treatment, remission or later rule change.

The scenario also omits:

- mortgage interest and rate-reset risk;
- valuation shortfall and required cash/CPF;
- legal, valuation and mortgage fees beyond the S$5,000 placeholder;
- property tax before and after occupation;
- maintenance, sinking fund and possible mixed-use cost-allocation disputes;
- furnishing and defects-period costs;
- sale legal fees and negotiated agency commission beyond the 2.5% planning allowance; and
- CPF accrued-interest and bank-redemption effects on cash released.

Cash returned at sale is not the same as economic profit. Loan-principal repayment and CPF refund reduce the completion cheque but are not themselves investment losses.

## Main risk register

### Price-premium risk

Pinery's captured average is about S$2,550 psf. Exact-size completed Tampines alternatives are often 30%–70% cheaper by quantum, while ParkTown's comparable new-launch sizes are generally 7%–10% cheaper on psf in the examples above. Future buyers must continue to value Pinery's direct link, mall and newness enough to preserve that premium.

### Completion and no-income risk

The contractual notice-of-vacant-possession date is October 2031. There is no rent before lawful possession, while progressive-payment financing accumulates. A portal's 2029/2030 completion badge is not a guarantee.

### Residual-stock selection risk

Public balance sheets concentrate on 4BR and 5BR stock, particularly 1,227 sqft homes. Residual concentration can identify simple product scarcity, but it can also reveal quantum resistance, an unpopular stack, floor or layout. A remaining unit must be compared with achieved same-type caveats, not just the project average.

### Completion-wave competition

Pinery will complete into a large island-wide supply pipeline. [URA's Q2 2026 statistics](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr26-57/) report about 60,600 private homes including ECs expected over coming years, with roughly 34,700 from 2029 onwards. ParkTown Residence, Aurelle and Rivelle also enlarge the Tampines new-home cohort. Supply does not predict a fall, but it weakens any assumption that every new landlord can achieve a scarcity rent at once.

### Mixed-use disamenity and governance risk

The mall adds convenience but also public footfall, operating hours, smells, deliveries, exhaust, refuse handling and signage. Confirm the affected stacks and whether the residential MCST is financially and operationally separated from the commercial component.

### Car and access risk

The published 474 residential lots are fewer than 588 homes. Direct MRT reduces car dependence but does not eliminate multi-car family demand, visitor parking or peak mall traffic. Verify the final parking allocation and access design.

### School-claim risk

Only an exact-postcode OneMap SchoolQuery result for the relevant year should be used for Primary 1 distance. St. Anthony's Canossian is specifically too close to the cutoff for a project-centroid claim.

### Market-cycle risk

The latest official data do not show uninterrupted OCR acceleration: URA reported OCR non-landed prices down 0.1% quarter on quarter in Q2 2026 and advised household prudence amid a large supply pipeline. A 2%–4% annual scenario is not an official forecast.

## Buyer action thresholds

1. **Obtain the developer-issued balance sheet.** Ignore portal card totals; confirm exact unit, type, price, discounts and option status on the same day.
2. **Anchor on achieved same-type caveats.** Compare the selected stack/floor with the full URA ledger, not the project average or lowest “from” price.
3. **Inspect the discount reason.** For a ground/PES or lower-PSF large unit, check public plaza, mall loading, road, MRT link, bin centre, transformer, facilities and privacy exposure.
4. **Price the hold to October 2031.** Run progressive-payment cash flow and interest at the offered rate plus at least a two-percentage-point stress.
5. **Use the exact school postcode.** Save the current OneMap result but recheck for the actual P1 exercise year.
6. **Do not underwrite a five-year S$500,000 gain.** Treat it as a strong-path outcome; the 3% five-year capital-only cases are about S$161,000–S$307,000 before financing and holding costs.
7. **For an investor, demand a margin of safety.** A lower psf due only to larger size is not enough; calculate rent, net yield, vacancy and future exit buyer quantum.
8. **For an owner-occupier, value utility explicitly.** If the direct link and mall save meaningful daily time for eight years or more, part of the premium is consumed as liveability rather than needing to reappear as profit.
