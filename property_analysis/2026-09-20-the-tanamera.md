## Decision

**The Tanamera is a large-layout own-stay candidate with a lease-constrained exit; do not pay for collective-sale speculation as though it were an agreed transaction.**

At the S$1,604,444 historical entry illustration, an unchanged exit price loses S$94,933; a 10% higher exit leaves +S$61,500 and a 10% lower exit leaves −S$251,366 before financing, ongoing property tax, maintenance, renovation and rent. These are total price changes with no forecast date. Own-stay utility can justify a purchase, but it should not be presented as a demonstrated investment return.

The subject's current-quarter project rent divided by this format's achieved sale PSF gives an aggregate gross-yield screen of about 3.1%. It combines different sale and rental homes; use an actual comparable lease and all recurring costs before relying on it.
