## Decision

Grandeur Park Residences is one of the stronger defensive resale propositions
around Tanah Merah MRT: it is comparatively young, close to the interchange,
has useful rental depth and recorded 33 resale caveats in the captured
publication cut from July 2025 through May 2026. Those qualities support
liquidity and ordinary appreciation.
They do **not** make every current advertisement cheap or establish
high-confidence “huge quantum” upside.

The best balanced family/investment cohort is the conventional **883–893 sqft
three-bedroom stock**. Latest achieved evidence includes S$1.695m–S$1.870m,
with a trailing-cohort median of S$1.768m. A disciplined buy box is therefore
about **S$1.74m–S$1.80m**, adjusted for floor, facing, renovation and tenancy.
The captured S$1.799m advertisement is supportable; S$1.86m–S$1.88m asks
require a demonstrably superior unit.

Other actionable reads are:

1. **Lowest-quantum lead:** 420–474 sqft only around S$870k–S$900k. The
   advertised 474 sqft at S$899,999 is plausible, not a proven bargain.
2. **Two-bedroom lead:** efficient 560–592 sqft stock around
   S$1.14m–S$1.23m. A S$1.10m ground-floor patio advertisement may offer
   headroom, but the patio/privacy trade-off must be priced explicitly.
3. **Larger two-bedroom/study lead:** 667 sqft around S$1.30m–S$1.36m.
4. **Large-family stock:** do not extrapolate from compact-unit PSF. The sole
   recent 1,238 sqft caveat was S$2.30m, so a S$2.70m ask needs exceptional
   stack-specific evidence. The 1,453 sqft S$3.088m ask is nearer its cohort
   evidence but is a high-quantum, narrower-exit purchase.
5. **Penthouse warning:** the 904 sqft two-bedroom penthouse near S$1.589m
   has a low-looking PSF partly because strata area and high-ceiling/roof
   utility differ from a conventional apartment. It is not a direct discount
   to an ordinary 883 sqft three-bedroom.

At a representative S$1.78m purchase of an 883 sqft unit, a simplified
capital-only profit of S$500,000 requires an exit near **S$2.404m**, or about
**S$2,722 psf**. That means approximately 6.19% annual appreciation over five
years or 3.83% over eight years, before financing and holding costs. Huge
upside is therefore possible only in a strong bull case or from an unusually
good entry—not the evidence-backed base case.

## Scope and evidence rules

This is a property analysis, not a factor-audit report and not a unified
property ranking. It keeps four evidence classes separate:

- **Advertised asks** show seller intent, not achieved value or confirmed
  availability.
- **URA-derived resale caveats** show lodged transactions, but caveats are
  voluntary and omit condition, facing, incentives and full contract terms.
- **URA-derived rental contracts** are area-band evidence, not proof of the
  rent for a particular sale unit.
- **Underwriting scenarios** are transparent assumptions, not forecasts.

The achieved-price dataset was refreshed through July 2026 from
[URA Property Market Information](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/property-market-information/pmiResidentialTransactionSearch).
Only rows explicitly tagged **Resale** are treated as resale evidence. URA
explains caveat and rental coverage in its
[REALIS methodology](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/reis/coverageandMethodology).
Public listings were swept on the research date and can be duplicated,
refreshed, withdrawn or repriced without notice.

## Project proposition

Grandeur Park is a 99-year leasehold project on Bedok South Avenue 3. Its lease
commenced in 2016, leaving approximately **88 years** at the research date.
The development has **720 residential apartments plus two retail units**;
portal totals that show 722 are counting the retail component. Construction
was completed around December 2020 and public portals commonly record TOP in
March 2021. The developer's corporate reporting is available through the
[Chip Eng Seng 2020 annual report](https://links.sgx.com/FileOpen/CES%20Annual%20Report%202020%20Final.ashx?App=Announcement&FileID=657483).

This is young enough that lease decay is not an immediate five-to-eight-year
constraint. It is nevertheless resale leasehold stock, not a new launch and
not freehold. A current buyer is paying after Tanah Merah accessibility,
facilities and most project execution risk have already crystallised.

A representative straight-line OneMap measurement from the development to
Tanah Merah MRT is approximately **220 metres**. It is a coordinate-to-
coordinate comparison, not a promised gate-to-platform walking distance.
Tanah Merah provides the East-West Line and the Changi Airport branch; the
interchange is a durable liveability and tenant-demand advantage.

## Location, catalysts and competing supply

- **MRT:** proximity to Tanah Merah interchange is delivered and durable, but
  already capitalised into resale prices. Inspect each stack for rail, road,
  station and aircraft noise rather than treating distance as purely positive.
- **Daily amenities:** Bedok Market Place, East Village and neighbourhood
  services broaden food and convenience options. Exact walkability depends on
  the development gate and crossing route.
- **Sceneca Residence:** the 268-home integrated development beside Tanah
  Merah MRT should improve station retail convenience. It also creates newer,
  directly linked competition; its launch/resale PSF is a price umbrella, not
  a guaranteed Grandeur convergence target.
- **Existing substitutes:** Urban Vista, Optima, The Glades, Casa Merah and
  the 748-home eCO provide abundant near-station alternatives. Freehold,
  mixed-use, age and layout differences prevent naive PSF blending.
- **Bedok Rise GLS:** URA awarded the site in December 2025 to Bellis
  Residential. The tender launch estimated approximately 380 homes, and
  [BCA's April 2026 contract list](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/338/e913f8bb-06d7-453b-8330-8f7ce707507e/free_pjt_list.pdf)
  later described five 12-storey blocks with 382 units. The final licensed
  sales schedule, marketing name, launch timing and TOP were unannounced at
  capture.
  [URA tender launch](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr25-48/);
  [URA award](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr25-66/).
- **Longer-run public realm:** Master Plan 2025 discusses green-mobility and
  recreation opportunities around the Tanah Merah viaduct and Changi depot.
  These are planning benefits with uncertain timing and unit-level
  capitalisation, not bankable appreciation.
  [URA East connectivity plan](https://www.ura.gov.sg/land-planning/master-plan/master-plan-2025/regional-plans/east/connecting-communities-with-places-to-unwind/).

The supply pipeline cuts both ways: new launches can establish higher
replacement-cost comparisons, but they also give future purchasers brand-new
alternatives. The conservative approach is not to underwrite automatic
convergence to future launch PSF.

## Five- and eight-year capital-only test

The illustration uses an **S$1.78m purchase of a conventional 883 sqft
three-bedroom** by a Singapore citizen buying a first residential property.
It assumes dutiable value equals the entry price; actual BSD and ABSD apply to
the higher of purchase consideration or market value.

Assumptions:

- residential BSD under current marginal tiers: **S$58,600**;
- acquisition legal/due-diligence allowance: **S$5,000**;
- day-one capital basis: **S$1,843,600**;
- net exit proceeds: **97.5% of exit price**, allowing 2.5% for sale agency,
  GST and legal friction;
- no ABSD and sale after the applicable SSD period; and
- no mortgage interest, maintenance, property tax, renovation, rental income
  or opportunity cost.

The formula is:

`capital profit = 0.975 × S$1.78m × (1 + annual growth)^years − S$1.8436m`.

| Annual price growth | Five-year exit | Five-year capital profit/loss | Eight-year exit | Eight-year capital profit/loss |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0% | S$1.780m | -S$108k | S$1.780m | -S$108k |
| 2% | S$1.965m | +S$73k | S$2.086m | +S$190k |
| 3% | S$2.064m | +S$168k | S$2.255m | +S$355k |
| 4% | S$2.166m | +S$268k | S$2.437m | +S$532k |
| 5% | S$2.272m | +S$371k | S$2.630m | +S$721k |

Capital break-even is an exit near **S$1.891m**, or about S$2,141 psf. A
S$500,000 capital-only gain needs about **S$2.404m/S$2,722 psf** and a
S$1.0m gain needs about **S$2.917m/S$3,303 psf**. Required annual growth is:

| Profit target | Required exit | Five-year annual growth | Eight-year annual growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Break-even | S$1.891m | 1.22% | 0.76% |
| S$500k | S$2.404m | 6.19% | 3.83% |
| S$1.0m | S$2.917m | 10.38% | 6.37% |

For a Singapore citizen buying a **second** residential property, current 20%
ABSD adds S$356,000. Simplified break-even rises to **S$2.256m**, approximately
S$2,555 psf, requiring about 4.85% annual appreciation over five years or
3.01% over eight years merely to recover acquisition and sale friction before
holding costs.

Duty references:
[IRAS BSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29),
[IRAS ABSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29)
and
[IRAS SSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property).
For residential property acquired on or after 4 July 2025, current SSD rates
are generally 16%, 12%, 8% and 4% for disposal within the first, second, third
and fourth year respectively, with no SSD only after the exact four-year
holding threshold. Buyer facts and current law must be checked at transaction.

## Risks and limits to upside

- **Current entry follows a large rerating:** median resale PSF has already
  risen materially since 2021.
- **Near-identical internal competition:** 720 homes and many repeated layouts
  mean several owners can compete at exit.
- **New supply is both benchmark and substitute:** Sceneca and the Bedok Rise
  parcel can support area visibility while drawing buyers toward newer stock.
- **Ground-floor and penthouse traps:** patio, roof, void or airspace area can
  distort PSF and reduce financing or exit comparability.
- **High-quantum liquidity:** 1,238–1,744 sqft stock has very thin recent
  evidence and a smaller buyer pool.
- **Leasehold tenure:** about 88 years is ample for this hold but cannot command
  freehold scarcity indefinitely.
- **Micro-location losses:** station/road/aircraft noise, west sun, privacy,
  blocked views and construction disruption vary by stack.
- **MCST and defects:** façade, waterproofing, pool, lift, air-conditioning and
  sinking-fund liabilities can offset the appeal of a young project.
- **Policy cost:** ABSD materially impairs a second-property investment case,
  and SSD removes short-term resale flexibility.

## Due diligence before an option to purchase

1. Confirm the exact block, unit, floor, stack, strata area, floor plan and
   seller-authorised executable price.
2. Reconcile duplicate advertisements by photographs, descriptions, tenancy,
   facing and agent authority; obtain same-day proof of availability.
3. Pull exact-size, same-floor-band and preferably same-stack caveats, then
   obtain a bank valuation.
4. Inspect useful internal area, balcony/patio/void allocation, west sun,
   privacy, station/rail/road/aircraft noise and future construction exposure
   at several times of day.
5. For a tenanted purchase, inspect the complete signed tenancy, stamp
   certificate, deposit, rent history, expiry, diplomatic/break clauses,
   inventory and repair obligations.
6. Review MCST accounts, AGM minutes, maintenance and sinking-fund amounts,
   insurance, defects, special levies, major contracts and renovation records.
7. Verify title, caveats, encumbrances, seller acquisition date and seller SSD.
8. Recalculate buyer-specific BSD, ABSD/remission, financing, TDSR, CPF/cash
   needs and interest-rate stress immediately before offer.

## Final assessment

Grandeur Park Residences has **high defensive quality, healthy liquidity and
moderate appreciation potential**. The 883–893 sqft three-bedroom cohort offers
the best balance of usable family space, tenant demand and resale evidence,
provided entry stays around matching caveats.

It does not have evidence-backed, project-wide huge quantum potential at
ordinary asks. A five-year S$500,000 capital-only gain from S$1.78m requires
about S$2,722 psf, well beyond the captured cohort range. An eight-year hold
makes that target more plausible, but still needs sustained 3.83% annual
growth before financing and holding costs.

The practical strategy is to buy a conventional, efficient layout below or
near exact cohort evidence, retain rental fallback, and hold beyond SSD. A
premium ask, unusual strata denominator or second-property ABSD turns a good
project into a weak investment quickly.
