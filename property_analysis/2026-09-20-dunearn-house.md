## Decision

At the S$2,000,500 historical entry illustration, an unchanged exit price loses S$124,638; a 10% higher exit leaves +S$70,411 and a 10% lower exit leaves −S$319,686 before financing, ongoing property tax, maintenance, renovation and rent. These are total price changes with no forecast date. Own-stay utility can justify a purchase, but it should not be presented as a demonstrated investment return.

The captured official 2026Q2 rental series does not establish a current subject rental median for this comparison. Rental fallback is unproven here; a missing statistic is neither zero rent nor a licence to substitute a neighbour's rent.

- URA developer return for 2026-08: 227 sold of 380 total homes, leaving 153 by arithmetic; 18 reported sold in that month. This is a dated aggregate balance, not an executable unit list.
- The August balance of 153 homes comprises 23 launched-unsold and 130 unlaunched homes; treating all 153 as failed-to-sell inventory would misread the release strategy.
- Its >70–100 sqm New Sale cohort has a S$2.944m median (N=82). Turf City’s 15,000–20,000-home public/private plan spans decades and represents future amenities, works and competition.
