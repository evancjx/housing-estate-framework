## Decision

Palmwoods has **credible low-quantum own-stay value and moderate resale
potential**, but not high-confidence “huge quantum” at the visible seller
expectations. The best apparent inspection lead is the approximately 1,033
sqft two-bedroom advertisement near **S$1.20m, or S$1,162 psf**. It sits below
the S$1,225–S$1,270 psf achieved by the four caveats captured from July 2025
to May 2026, although those caveats are different sizes and may have different
condition, floor and tenancy.

For a family-sized 1,141–1,207 sqft three-bedroom, an evidence-led offer around
**S$1.45m–S$1.52m** is more defensible than the visible S$1.58m–S$1.60m asks.
The latest 1,292 sqft caveat achieved S$1.64m in May 2026, but a buyer should
not transfer that price blindly to a smaller, lower-quality or tenanted unit.

The conditional entry ladder is:

1. **At or below S$1.45m for roughly 1,100–1,300 sqft:** potentially compelling
   if condition, strata area, remaining lease, noise and valuation are clean.
2. **S$1.45m–S$1.52m:** defensible for an ordinary three-bedroom with a
   functional plan.
3. **S$1.52m–S$1.60m:** pay only for verified renovation, superior aspect,
   floor, tenancy terms or uncommon usable area.
4. **Above S$1.60m:** weak investment entry without a materially larger format
   or exceptional attributes.

The S$1.20m advertisement is an **ask, not an achieved bargain**. It requires
agent confirmation that the card is current, unique and accurately reports
1,033 sqft. Portal pages expose inconsistent raw-card counts and one
S$1.49m card alternately described around 1,001–1,002 sqft and as two or three
bedrooms.

Under a representative S$1.50m family-unit purchase, an **SC first-property
buyer** starts with about **S$1.5496m** of acquisition basis after S$44,600
BSD and S$5,000 legal/technical allowance. A S$500,000 capital-only profit
requires an exit near **S$2.102m** after 2.5% selling friction—about 6.98%
annual sale-price growth over five years or 4.31% over eight years. That is a
bull outcome, not the base case.

Here, “huge quantum” means a credible large absolute-dollar gain after purchase
and sale friction, not a low headline ticket or a high percentage quoted from
an advertisement.

## Scope and evidence rules

This is a property analysis, not a factor-audit report or a cross-tenure
ranking. It separates:

- **Advertised inventory:** evidence of public marketing, not proof that a
  unit is unique, available or saleable at the stated area and price.
- **Achieved evidence:** URA-derived resale caveats captured in the repository;
  caveats are not valuations and may not expose condition or exact plan.
- **Rental evidence:** observed contracts by broad size band, not guaranteed
  future rent or net yield.
- **Scenario underwriting:** transparent arithmetic, not a forecast, bank
  valuation or licensed appraisal.

The primary official reference is
[URA Property Market Information](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/property-market-information/pmiResidentialTransactionSearch).
The free search covers the latest 60 months. Resale data are caveats lodged
with SLA; caveat lodging is voluntary, so the ledger is not exhaustive.
[URA REALIS coverage and methodology](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/reis/coverageandMethodology)
explains the broader data basis.

## Capital-only return test

The representative illustration assumes:

- purchase price: **S$1.500m**;
- buyer: **Singapore citizen (SC) buying a first residential property**, the
  SC first-property baseline with no ABSD;
- BSD: **S$44,600**, computed under
  [current IRAS residential BSD tiers](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29);
- acquisition legal/technical allowance: **S$5,000**;
- base acquisition basis: **S$1.5496m**;
- exit proceeds: **97.5% of sale price**, a 2.5% selling-friction allowance;
  and
- no financing, maintenance, tax, renovation, vacancy or rent in the profit
  figure.

The scenario assumes dutiable value equals the S$1.500m entry price. Actual
BSD and ABSD are charged on the **higher of consideration or market value**,
so an apparently discounted purchase can attract duty on a higher value.

The formula is:

`capital-only profit = 0.975 × future sale price − acquisition basis`

| Annual nominal sale-price change | Five-year exit / profit | Eight-year exit / profit |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1% | S$1.577m / **−S$12k** | S$1.624m / **S$34k** |
| 3% | S$1.739m / **S$146k** | S$1.900m / **S$303k** |
| 5% | S$1.914m / **S$317k** | S$2.216m / **S$611k** |

Break-even is approximately **S$1.589m**. A S$500,000 capital-only target
requires about **S$2.102m**, before holding and finance costs. The 5%/eight-year
row clears that target, but it is a scenario rather than evidence that the
market will deliver it.

ABSD changes the result materially.
[IRAS's current ABSD table](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29)
charges a Singapore citizen 20% when buying a second residential property.
An additional S$300,000 raises friction-only break-even to about **S$1.897m**,
before financing and holding costs. Buyer profile and any remission must be
confirmed independently.

For residential property bought on or after 4 July 2025,
[IRAS's current SSD schedule](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property)
applies 16%, 12%, 8% and 4% within the first four holding years. The five- and
eight-year illustrations assume sale after the exact four-year anniversary and
therefore no SSD under the current schedule.

## Due diligence before an offer

1. Obtain dated written confirmation that the advertisement is live and unique;
   reconcile block, floor, stack, bedrooms and strata area.
2. Match the subsidiary strata certificate, floor plan and option to the card;
   identify balconies, terraces, voids or altered walls.
3. Request the latest AGM minutes, audited accounts, maintenance and
   sinking-fund balances, insurance, by-laws and planned special levies.
4. Inspect roof and wet-area waterproofing, windows, pipes, electrical load,
   air-conditioning ledges, pests and signs of settlement or water ingress.
5. Visit at peak and late-night periods to test Upper Changi Road, MRT and
   aircraft noise by stack.
6. Confirm current occupancy, lease terms, deposit transfer, vacant possession
   and whether the stated tenancy ending in 2027 applies.
7. Obtain an independent bank indication and valuation before paying a
   non-refundable amount.
8. Recompute BSD, ABSD, CPF usage, loan limits and SSD dates for the actual
   buyers and contract date.
9. Walk the exact gate-to-platform route and test daily amenity access rather
   than relying on portal minutes.
10. Model renovation, maintenance, property tax, insurance, vacancy and
    financing separately; none is included in the capital-only table.

## Final assessment

Palmwoods' strongest thesis is **selective value in an older, low-rise family
format**, not automatic free appreciation. The S$1.20m card deserves immediate
verification; the 1,141–1,207 sqft three-bedrooms become more interesting near
S$1.45m–S$1.52m. At ordinary S$1.58m–S$1.60m asks, the evidence supports
own-stay utility and moderate upside, not high-confidence huge quantum.
