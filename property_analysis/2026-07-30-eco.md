## Decision

eCO has a **relatively young lease and credible future-rail proposition**, but
it still does not offer high-confidence huge quantum at ordinary current asks.
Its 2012 lease, February 2017 TOP and proximity to both Tanah Merah MRT and the
future Bedok South MRT support a longer resale runway. The complication is
that eCO contains five materially different housing formats. Compact Suites or
SOHOs, conventional apartments, Lofts, patio formats and strata Townhouses
cannot be valued from one project PSF.

The best balanced buy box is a conventional approximately **839–861 sqft
two-bedroom around S$1.40m–S$1.45m**. Recent representative achieved evidence
includes S$1.385m for 840 sqft in April 2026 and S$1.470m for 861 sqft in March.
A current 861 sqft ask around S$1.445m sits inside that range rather than below
it.

The conditional entry ladder is:

1. **Conventional 839–861 sqft:** at or below S$1.40m is attractive;
   S$1.41m–S$1.47m is fair; above S$1.48m needs a superior floor, stack,
   renovation or tenancy.
2. **Compact 549–603 sqft Suite/SOHO:** at or below S$920k is attractive;
   S$921k–S$950k is evidence-aligned; above S$960k needs exact-format support.
3. **Approximately 1,012–1,033 sqft family stock:** underwrite from its own
   S$1.58m–S$1.81m recent caveats, not from compact-unit PSF.
4. **Loft, patio and Townhouse stock:** obtain the strata plan and value usable
   internal area, outdoor/void area and landed-like utility separately.

At a S$1.40m representative entry, eight-year 3% annual price growth produces
about **S$284k capital-only profit** before financing and holding costs. A S$1m
capital-only profit requires an exit near **S$2.508m**. That is not a plausible
base case for an ordinary 840 sqft unit. eCO offers **moderate, comparatively
better-quality growth potential**, not huge risk-adjusted quantum.

## Scope and evidence rules

This is a property analysis, not a factor-audit report or unified ranking. It
separates:

- **Advertised asks:** marketing evidence that can be duplicated, stale or
  withdrawn.
- **Achieved transactions and rents:** caveat or contract evidence, without
  complete condition and incentive data.
- **Scenario underwriting:** mathematical sensitivities, not a forecast.

The primary transaction reference is
[URA Property Market Information](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/property-market-information/pmiResidentialTransactionSearch).
Caveats are voluntary and not exhaustive. At eCO, exact strata area and the
legal unit type are essential because portal bedroom labels frequently
misclassify compact, SOHO and loft stock.

five lifestyles: SOHO, Suite, Loft, Condominium and Townhouse. The same official
page contains an internal unit-count conflict: its narrative says **748
units**, while the fact sheet says **752**. Current project databases and a
later townhouse breakdown support 748, but title and MCST records should settle
the legal count.

The captured URA records state a lease commencing in 2012, leaving roughly
**84–85 years** at the research date. Lease decay is not an immediate
five-to-eight-year constraint.

The design includes a green corridor, conserved trees and a rain garden.
Variety is an amenity and a valuation risk: many close internal substitutes can
appear at once, and share values, maintenance exposure and buyer profiles differ
across typologies.

## Townhouse warning

[EdgeProp's June 2026 mortgagee-sale report](https://www.edgeprop.sg/property-news/rare-strata-townhouse-bedok-offered-mortgagee-sale-378-mil)
describes 714 apartments and 34 intermediate strata Townhouses within the
748-unit project, and a townhouse guide price of S$3.78m. A mortgagee guide is
not an achieved price. The report confirms scarcity and legal typology, not
automatic value.

Townhouses have a narrower buyer and financing pool, materially higher total
quantum and different outdoor/void-area economics. Their low headline PSF is
not evidence that conventional apartments are overpriced or that the
Townhouses are cheap.

## Rental fallback

The
[99.co project rental ledger](https://www.99.co/singapore/condos-apartments/eco)
shows recent compact 500–700 sqft rents mostly around S$2,350–S$3,250 monthly,
conventional 800–900 sqft two-bedrooms around **S$3,600–S$3,800**, and
1,000–1,100 sqft three-bedrooms around S$4,600–S$5,000.

Using S$3,700 monthly against a S$1.40m conventional-unit purchase gives
approximately **3.17% gross yield**. This excludes vacancy, maintenance,
agent fees, property tax, repairs, furnishing, insurance and financing. Compact
units can show higher gross PSF rent but also face more internal competition
and turnover.

## Location and pipeline

eCO has two transport propositions. Tanah Merah MRT is already operating; the
actual walking route varies by block and gate. Bedok South MRT on TEL Stage 5
was still a future station at the capture timestamp. LTA stated that final
integration testing was under way before
[TEL5 opening in the second half of 2026](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/4/news-releases/train-service-adjustments-tel-and-dtl-to-facilitate-rail-expansion-works.html).
Do not advertise it as open until LTA confirms operations.

URA awarded the
[Bayshore Drive mixed-use site](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr26-55/)
on 20 July 2026 to Gemini Residential and Gemini Trustee for S$2.128 billion.
[URA's launch details](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr26-23/) describe a
potential 1,280-home development integrated with Bedok South MRT, a bus
interchange and retail. That can improve amenities and validate the rail node
while adding substantial newer competition. The 99-year
[Bedok Rise site award](https://www.ura.gov.sg/news/media/pr25-66/) similarly
adds future Tanah Merah-area supply.

Test the exact route to both stations and rail, road and aircraft noise at the
subject stack. No school-admission premium is assumed without an exact-block,
current official distance check.

## Underwriting and future quantum

The representative case is a conventional 839–861 sqft two-bedroom at
**S$1.40m**. BSD is **S$40,600** under current
[IRAS residential BSD tiers](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29).
The illustration assumes dutiable value equals entry price; actual BSD and
ABSD apply to the higher of purchase consideration or market value.
The no-ABSD case assumes a Singapore citizen buying a first residential
property.

`capital-only profit = exit price × 0.975 − entry price − BSD − S$5,000 acquisition legal/technical allowance`

The assumed 2.5% selling friction and fixed S$5,000 acquisition allowance are
included. All scenarios exclude any other legal fees, financing, maintenance,
renovation, property tax, rent, vacancy and opportunity cost. The
second-property sensitivity deducts **20% of entry**, the current
[SC second-property ABSD rate](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29).

| Hold / annual price growth | Exit quantum | SC first-property capital-only profit | SC second-property after 20% ABSD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 years / 0% | S$1.400m | -S$81k | -S$361k |
| 5 years / 2% | S$1.546m | S$61k | -S$219k |
| 5 years / 3% | S$1.623m | S$137k | -S$143k |
| 5 years / 4% | S$1.703m | S$215k | -S$65k |
| 8 years / 0% | S$1.400m | -S$81k | -S$361k |
| 8 years / 2% | S$1.640m | S$154k | -S$126k |
| 8 years / 3% | S$1.773m | S$284k | S$4k |
| 8 years / 4% | S$1.916m | S$422k | S$142k |

Capital-only break-even is about 1.2% annual growth over five years or 0.7%
over eight before holding costs. The current
[four-year SSD schedule](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property)
for purchases on or after 4 July 2025 does not affect modelled exits if dates
and rules remain unchanged.

## Catalysts

- Younger lease and building than the nearby 1990s and 1980s controls.
- TEL5 and Bedok South node formation can broaden tenant and buyer access.
- A large project produces regular caveats and multiple entry tickets.
- Conventional 2BR total quantum remains below newer family-sized alternatives.

## Risks

- Typology confusion can create false bargains based on low PSF or a wrong
  bedroom label.
- Large internal supply can cap seller pricing, especially in compact stock.
- TEL5 and Bayshore benefits are partly anticipated and accompany future new
  supply.
- Complex landscaping, water features, conserved greenery and mixed formats
  may require higher long-term maintenance; inspect MCST budgets.
- Townhouses and special layouts have narrow exit pools.
- No current collective-sale tender was verified; assign zero en-bloc value.

## Due diligence before an offer

1. Obtain title, strata plan, approved floor plan, legal typology and share
   value.
2. Measure the split of interior, balcony, patio, void and roof area.
3. Review MCST accounts, AGM minutes, sinking fund, landscape and water-feature
   maintenance, waterproofing claims, defects, major works and levies.
4. Confirm station routes, tenancy, vacant possession and renovation
   approvals.
5. Obtain a bank valuation and compare the subject only with same-format,
   same-area caveats.

## Final assessment

eCO is a **conditional buy around S$1.40m–S$1.45m for a conventional
839–861 sqft unit**, and potentially around S$900k–S$940k for the right compact
format. Its younger lease and future dual-station context are genuine
advantages. The investable edge is format-specific pricing discipline, not a
project-wide PSF convergence story or a Townhouse-style quantum windfall.
