### How much the owner can earn

This section uses the report's common capital-only convention so the result is consistent with the other project cases:

- owner is a Singapore citizen who bought a first residential property in August 2023 and paid no ABSD;
- original acquisition basis is purchase price + 2023 BSD + S$5,000 legal/technical allowance;
- net exit proceeds are 97.5% of gross price, allowing 2.5% for selling agency, GST/legal and possible assignment friction;
- profit is net exit proceeds less acquisition basis; finance, progressive-payment interest, maintenance, tax, insurance and opportunity cost are excluded.

### Tax, ABSD and possible pre-title sale effects

An August 2023 acquisition remains under the **three-year SSD schedule** for properties bought from 11 March 2017 through 3 July 2025. IRAS says no SSD applies when the disposal is on or after the exact third anniversary. A legally effective September 2026 disposal should therefore have **zero SSD**, but the solicitor must verify the original acquisition date and controlling disposal date. The current four-year schedule for purchases from 4 July 2025 does not apply retrospectively. See the current [IRAS SSD rules](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property) and [IRAS declaration notes](https://www.iras.gov.sg/media/docs/default-source/uploadedfiles/pdf/declaration-form-ssd-for-residential-properties.pdf?sfvrsn=eef90150_16); the disposal date is generally when the buyer exercises the OTP or signs the S&P, not the later completion date.

At the research timestamp, no TOP/VP notice, issued title, achieved project rent or owner resale was located. If title has not issued at disposal, CEA's [uncompleted-property seller checklist](https://www.cea.gov.sg/docs/default-source/Professionals/Agreements-Checklists/checklist-for-seller-%28sale-of-uncompleted-private-residential-property%29_jan21.pdf) requires attention to mortgage/CPF redemption, negative sale and developer notification, while its [standard private-property agreement](https://www.cea.gov.sg/docs/default-source/Professionals/Agreements-Checklists/agreement-for-the-sale-and-purchase-of-private-residential-property-2021.pdf) provides for assignment of the vendor's S&P. ## Scope and evidence rules

This is a **property analysis**, not a factor audit or a ranking within the estate framework. It assesses the project and the units publicly marketed at the research timestamp.

Evidence is separated as follows:

- **Official/legal facts:** the developer's legal project footer, Wing Tai releases, URA, LTA, NParks, JLD government pages and IRAS.
- **Achieved transaction evidence:** 304 raw URA-source New Sale records in the committed D22 extract, a 302-row exact-address EdgeProp cross-check, and 287 conservatively deduplicated rows in `data/inputs/ura_private.csv`.
- **Advertised evidence:** current portal cards and sales-microsite balance tables. These are asks and marketing claims, not achieved prices or proof of availability.
- **Scenarios:** transparent capital-only illustrations, not forecasts.

The [URA transaction search](https://eservice.ura.gov.sg/property-market-information/pmiResidentialTransactionSearch) explains that New Sale records from 25 May 2015 are based on Options to Purchase issued by developers. Exact dates quoted here are EdgeProp's published **Date of Sale** for URA-sourced developer submissions; they are not caveat-lodgement, S&P-execution or legal-completion dates. URA data can also lag, include reissued options or fail to reveal cancellations.

The terms **huge potential quantum** and **S$500,000 target** mean a nominal S$500,000 capital profit after current BSD, applicable ABSD, S$5,000 legal/technical allowance and 2.5% assumed exit friction. They do not mean S$500,000 cash-on-cash profit, and they exclude finance, progressive-payment interest, maintenance, property tax, insurance, vacancy, renovation, rent and opportunity cost. A high purchase quantum can make a nominal S$500,000 outcome look easier while producing a mediocre return on equity.

## Capital-only potential-quantum test

### Common assumptions

The base case is a **Singapore citizen buying a first residential property**, with no ABSD. Current [IRAS BSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29) and [ABSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29) apply to the higher of consideration or market value.

- Acquisition basis = purchase price + BSD + applicable ABSD + S$5,000 legal/technical allowance.
- Net exit proceeds = 97.5% of gross exit price, assuming 2.5% selling/legal friction.
- Profit = net exit proceeds minus acquisition basis.
- Breakeven exit = acquisition basis / 0.975.
- S$500,000 target exit = (acquisition basis + S$500,000) / 0.975.
- S$1,000,000 target exit = (acquisition basis + S$1,000,000) / 0.975.

All cases assume the dutiable market value equals the stated consideration. A higher assessed value raises duties, acquisition basis and target exits.

The five- and eight-year horizons run from an assumed August 2026 entry, not from TOP. Five years reaches about August 2031 and supplies only about four years of completed-project operation if expected VP occurs in August 2027. Progressive-payment financing and all ownership/rental cash flows remain excluded.

For acquisitions on or after 4 July 2025, current [IRAS SSD](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property) is generally 16%, 12%, 8% and 4% for disposal within the first, second, third and fourth holding years respectively. The illustrations assume exact exit dates are **after** the current four-year SSD window.

## Due diligence before an option or booking

1. Obtain a **developer-issued, dated** balance-unit chart and price-list PDF showing exact unit number, block, floor, stack, net price, discounts, incentives, reservation status and expiry.
2. Establish whether the transaction is a **developer New Sale or private subsale**. For a subsale, have solicitors verify seller authority, original S&P, assignment/subsale mechanics, outstanding progressive payments and completion obligations.
3. Reconcile every portal card with the exact unit number. Reject generic “last unit,” “direct developer” or freshly refreshed cards that cannot do so.
4. Confirm dutiable market value, buyer profile, BSD, ABSD, CPF use, financing and exact SSD dates with the buyer's solicitor/tax adviser.
5. Review the actual S&P expected-VP date, permitted extensions, liquidated damages, legal-completion date and current construction progress.
6. Obtain the approved floor plan and strata breakdown. Separate bedrooms/living area from stairs, voids, terraces, balconies, air-con ledges and other low-utility area.
7. For dual-key use, verify the approved configuration, privacy, services, fire egress and whether the intended occupancy/letting arrangement is lawful and practical.
8. For a ground-floor unit, inspect privacy, drainage, moisture, pests, garden/event noise, security, refuse/loading routes and flood pathways after rain.
9. For a lake/garden-facing or penthouse unit, test afternoon sun, heat load, glare, wind, roof waterproofing, plant noise, lightning protection and exact sightlines from habitable rooms.
10. Walk each gate to Lakeside and Chinese Garden MRT in heat and rain. Confirm the claimed garden side gate, public-opening constraints and step-free routes.
11. Obtain estimated maintenance contributions, sinking-fund treatment, property tax, insurance, furnishing and defect-remediation budgets.
12. Re-underwrite rent only after comparing achieved 2027–2028 leases in LakeGarden, SORA and completed station-area projects; apply vacancy and agent/maintenance/tax costs.
13. Stress an exit against completed Lakeville, Lakefront, Caspian and J Gateway alternatives, plus Lucerne Grand, rather than assuming only premium new-launch buyers.
14. For the S$4.28m formats, ask agents for evidence of actual D22 buyer depth above S$5m and expected marketing duration—not merely PSF comparisons.

## Final assessment

The LakeGarden Residences has a **high-quality, differentiated own-stay proposition**: a new Wing Tai development beside the completed Jurong Lake Gardens, with long-range JLD investment around it. That utility is real. It is offset by an approximately one-kilometre MRT walk, no rent until expected 2027 VP, no captured achieved secondary-market exit evidence, and substantial nearby supply.

For a Singapore-citizen first-property buyer:

- The **S$1.520 million ground-floor 678 sq ft card, with sale type unverified,** is the only current card with a visible achieved-evidence discount. It is worth diligence, not an immediate offer. Its low quantum does not support a five-year S$500,000 base case.
- A genuinely available **ordinary 1,238 sq ft unit near S$2.700 million** could be defensible at the lower end of matched evidence, but the current card appears to mirror a sold record. It must not be called live without a different exact unit.
- Between equal-priced penthouses, the **2,153 sq ft dual-key** is the better first screen on stated area. A S$500,000 nominal outcome is mathematically plausible at moderate CAGR, but requires a future sale above S$5.1 million and risks S$4.28 million today. That is not outsized risk-adjusted potential.

For a Singapore-citizen second-property buyer, 20% ABSD destroys most base-case capital outcomes. Other citizenship and ownership profiles require separate current-duty analysis.

**Verdict: selective buy for verified unit-specific utility or a genuine discount; no project-wide huge-potential-quantum thesis.** The next move is not to chase 65 or 105 cards. It is to obtain the developer's dated balance sheet, prove the S$1.588 million private-subsale claim and establish the S$1.520 million card's sale type, inspect the exact unit, and price the offer against achieved matched-size evidence.
