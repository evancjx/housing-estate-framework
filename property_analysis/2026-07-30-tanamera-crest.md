## Decision

Tanamera Crest is a **fair-value family condominium with useful rental
fallback**, but current evidence does not support high-confidence huge quantum.
Its 288-unit scale, approximately 73 remaining lease years and ordinary
1,163–1,216 sqft three-bedroom stock around S$1.37m–S$1.49m provide a
manageable family ticket. Public sale-card supply is visibly high relative to
recent achieved turnover, so buyers should negotiate rather than chase.

The strongest buy box is approximately **S$1.37m–S$1.42m for a normal
1,173–1,206 sqft three-bedroom**. Nine recent achieved resales in the
1,001–1,216 sqft band range from S$1.16m to S$1.488m, with a S$1.40m median.
The compact 1,001 sqft and ordinary 1,180–1,206 sqft layouts should not be
blended blindly.

The conditional entry ladder is:

1. **At or below S$1.40m:** attractive to fair for an ordinary
   1,173–1,206 sqft unit if floor, condition and stack are sound.
2. **S$1.41m–S$1.46m:** evidence-backed for a better floor, facing,
   renovation or vacant possession.
3. **S$1.47m–S$1.50m:** upper recent territory; require a strong
   unit-specific reason.
4. **Above S$1.50m:** premium pricing for the ordinary three-bedroom segment.

Approximately 1,001 sqft two-bedroom and 2,013 sqft four-bedroom stock require
separate ladders. Current asks around S$1.20m–S$1.25m and S$2.30m–S$2.45m
respectively are not direct controls for the core three-bedroom.

At a S$1.40m representative entry, eight-year 3% annual price growth gives
about **S$284k capital-only profit** before financing and holding costs. A S$1m
capital-only profit needs an exit near **S$2.508m**. That is not supported as a
base case for an ordinary three-bedroom. Potential quantum is **moderate, not
huge**.

## Underwriting and future quantum

The representative case is an ordinary approximately 1,173–1,206 sqft
three-bedroom at **S$1.40m**. BSD is **S$40,600** under current
[IRAS residential BSD tiers](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28bsd%29).
The illustration assumes dutiable value equals entry price; actual BSD and
ABSD apply to the higher of purchase consideration or market value.
The no-ABSD case assumes a Singapore citizen buying a first residential
property.

`capital-only profit = exit price × 0.975 − entry price − BSD − S$5,000 acquisition legal/technical allowance`

The assumed 2.5% selling friction and fixed S$5,000 acquisition allowance are
included. Scenarios exclude any other legal fees, financing, maintenance,
renovation, property tax, rent, vacancy and opportunity cost. The
second-property sensitivity deducts **20% of entry** under the current
[SC second-property ABSD rate](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/additional-buyer%27s-stamp-duty-%28absd%29).

| Hold / annual price growth | Exit quantum | SC first-property capital-only profit | SC second-property after 20% ABSD |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 years / 0% | S$1.400m | -S$81k | -S$361k |
| 5 years / 2% | S$1.546m | S$61k | -S$219k |
| 5 years / 3% | S$1.623m | S$137k | -S$143k |
| 5 years / 4% | S$1.703m | S$215k | -S$65k |
| 8 years / 0% | S$1.400m | -S$81k | -S$361k |

| 8 years / 4% | S$1.916m | S$422k | S$142k |

Capital-only break-even is about 1.2% annually over five years or 0.7% over
eight before holding costs. Current
[SSD rules](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/selling-or-disposing-property/seller%27s-stamp-duty-%28ssd%29-for-residential-property)
apply for four years to purchases from 4 July 2025; modelled exits avoid SSD
only if dates and rules remain unchanged.

## Catalysts

- Conventional three-bedroom quantum around S$1.4m has a broad family buyer
  pool.
- Future TEL5 and Bedok South retail/transport integration can improve access.
- Recent caveats provide a clear negotiation anchor.
- Approximately 73 lease years offer a better runway than the older Tanamera

## Risks

- Visible raw listing supply is high relative to nine recent resales; duplicates
  must be removed before interpreting competition.
- Approximately 72–73 lease years remain now and 64–65 after eight years.
- Ageing lifts, pipes, façades, waterproofing, roofs, pool plant and carpark can
  create special levies.
- New Bedok South and Tanah Merah projects are amenity catalysts and newer
  substitutes.
- Larger 2,000 sqft stock has a narrower exit pool and should not set ordinary
  unit PSF.
- No current collective-sale tender was verified; en-bloc has zero base-case
  value.

## Due diligence before an offer

1. Obtain title, exact lease date, legal TOP/CSC, strata plan, floor plan, share
   value and renovation approvals.
2. Review MCST accounts and AGM minutes, sinking fund, insurance claims,
   planned major works and special levies.
3. Inspect waterproofing, roof, façade, plumbing, lifts, pool and carpark.
4. Verify tenancy, vacant possession, station routes and noise.
5. Obtain written bank valuation and compare same-size, similar-floor caveats
   rather than portal project medians.

## Final assessment

Tanamera Crest is a rational **family own-stay or rental-fallback buy around
S$1.37m–S$1.42m for an ordinary 1,173–1,206 sqft unit**. Clear recent caveats
and manageable quantum are strengths. The correct thesis is moderate,
entry-led appreciation plus future transport convenience—not huge quantum or
automatic convergence to newer Bedok South supply.
