## Decision

**Treat Rivelle as an eligibility- and availability-constrained EC choice; its historical developer price is a useful benchmark but not an open-market investment entry.**

At the S$1,815,000 historical entry illustration, an unchanged exit price loses S$110,725; a 10% higher exit leaves +S$66,238 and a 10% lower exit leaves −S$287,688 before financing, ongoing property tax, maintenance, renovation and rent. These are total price changes with no forecast date. Own-stay utility can justify a purchase, but it should not be presented as a demonstrated investment return.

The captured official 2026Q2 rental series does not establish a current subject rental median for this comparison. Rental fallback is unproven here; a missing statistic is neither zero rent nor a licence to substitute a neighbour's rent.

- URA developer return for 2026-08: 558 sold of 572 total homes, leaving 14 by arithmetic; 6 reported sold in that month. This is a dated aggregate balance, not an executable unit list.
- The dominant >70–100 sqm New Sale cohort has 371 records at S$1.815m median. Its larger >100–130 sqm cohort is S$2.272m (N=179), so family quantum changes materially with area.
