## Decision

**Urban Vista is a lower-entry Tanah Merah option when the specific conventional layout works; its low quantum and heterogeneous formats must not be mistaken for a uniform valuation gap.**

At the S$925,000 historical entry illustration, an unchanged exit price loses S$50,475; a 10% higher exit leaves +S$39,712 and a 10% lower exit leaves −S$140,662 before financing, ongoing property tax, maintenance, renovation and rent. These are total price changes with no forecast date. Own-stay utility can justify a purchase, but it should not be presented as a demonstrated investment return.

The subject's current-quarter project rent divided by this format's achieved sale PSF gives an aggregate gross-yield screen of about 3.9%. It combines different sale and rental homes; use an actual comparable lease and all recurring costs before relying on it.

## Ownership restrictions and project risks

- Paying for extra perceived loft utility may not be supported by valuation or the next buyer.
- A low entry price makes a very large absolute-dollar profit require a demanding percentage gain.
- Actual TOP is not independently verified here. An expected completion date or sale category does not resolve it.
- The offered unit's floor plan, usable space, orientation, defects, major works and management-fund position are not established by a transaction record.
- Current listing exposure and executable seller terms are not captured consistently; asking stock is not inferred from past transactions.


## Conditions for proceeding

1. Verify approved internal/loft area and bank-recognised usable space before comparing PSF.
2. For an ordinary compact home, use the exact registered-size owner records and check floor, facing and tenancy.
3. Match the offered home to its own sale/tenure/area cell, starting with the 11-record >50–70 sqm (538–753 sqft) reference at S$925,000 only if that is the required format. Explain differences in floor, layout and condition before setting an offer.
4. Test the actual quotation against acquisition costs and a conservative exit. The selected historical entry needs S$976,769 merely to recover the modeled purchase and selling costs; holding expenses raise that requirement.
5. For own stay, verify the actual daily journey, space and holding horizon; for investment, obtain an achievable lease and calculate net carry after all costs.
6. Confirm title, lease, current sale status and applicable purchaser/holding restrictions before treating any historical price as a buyable opportunity.
