What this comparison can—and cannot—say

Recorded cohort

Same size is a filter, not a floor plan

The cohort includes achieved transactions whose recorded area falls within your tolerance. Similar area does not establish the same layout, bedroom count, floor, facing, condition or stack.

Explicit assumptions

A projected scenario, not a forecast

Growth and selling costs are user inputs. Outputs show scenario arithmetic around recorded evidence; they do not predict a future valuation or return.

Market boundary

No liquidity claim

Transaction count describes recorded depth only. It does not estimate time to sell, buyer demand at your price, or whether a future sale will complete.

Evidence and coverage

Project identity, tenure, straight-line station diagnostics and primary-school radius evidence come from the committed comparison catalog. Transaction rows are fetched from the committed shard named for each project. Core metrics use canonical URA transactions from the latest 60 complete months. The incomplete historical backfill stays separately labelled and is not blended into those metrics; the known January–May 2021 gap is not filled.
