How screening, profile scores, Value evidence and time horizons work

Filters before scores

Hard constraints determine eligibility first. Filtered choices have no public score or rank on this page, even though diagnostic component data may exist upstream.

Experimental profile score

The score combines available Liveability, Value, employment, lease, Provision and life-path inputs. Missing inputs are omitted and weights renormalised; coverage is therefore part of the result. Scenario weight entries update model defaults rather than replacing them.

Value and tenure

HDB, condominium and landed evidence are separate. Direct, proxy and unavailable bases stay visible. Below 100 transactions, Value is published as a band only; because the composite uses Value, its public score and rank are also withheld and the remaining reportable choices are re-ranked locally.

Time limitations

Liveability uses the stated T0, T5 or T15 horizon, while employment remains current/T0. Life paths are T0→T5 diagnostics and can overlap the selected T5 cell; treat the composite as a screening aid, not an independent forecast.

Decision boundary: use this page to form a scenario-specific shortlist. Financing, eligibility, project quality, unit layout, school-distance eligibility and personal care anchors still require household- and unit-level checks.
