    Read the framework correctly

    Three views, three questions

    Provision · What is here?

    Objective, supply-side and comparable across estates. It produces one estate score and an archetype.

    Liveability · Does it fit me?

    Person-relative and horizon-specific. It is a 4-persona × 3-horizon matrix, not a universal ranking.

    Value · Is the cost justified for me?

    Lives inside Liveability and remains segmented by tenure. HDB and private are never blended.

    About exact units: Public EdgeProp transaction rows commonly mask unit numbers as #XX-XX or preserve only a floor band. Masked values are not exact unit identifiers; exact-unit claims require an authenticated, verifiable source.
