  • Prices, months, floor bands and sqm come from URA's transaction API, which covers the last five years. Shaded rows are older sales from EdgeProp or PropertyNoob and are not verified against that API.
  • URA's API gives only the month. Exact dates, blocks and floors come from EdgeProp, which republishes URA's dated record with the stack number hidden.
  • Unit numbers are not official. Published: PropertyNoob printed the unit with this price, size, floor and date. Determined by block, floor and size: only one unit on that block and floor has this size. Inferred: the unit chart's sold date, or the developer's recent-sales list, picks one unit on that block and floor. By elimination: New Sales only; the last sold unit of that size and floor without a New Sale. Ambiguous: more than one unit fits, and the candidates are listed.
  • $psf = price ÷ sqft. Prices are gross; URA publishes no nett price. Recent months may be incomplete because URA registers transactions with a lag.
