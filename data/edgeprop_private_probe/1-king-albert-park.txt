Description
1 King Albert Park is a freehold condominium completed in 1997. The development occupies a land area of 8,926 sqm and a Gross Floor Area of 14,460 sqm. It consists of 101 units. According to records, buyers of this development are Singaporean 81.0%, PR 8.5%, Foreigner 6.3%, Company 2.6%.

1 KING ALBERT PARK is located along King Albert Park in Bukit Timah, in District 21. It is accessible through the nearest MRTs including King Albert Park DT6 and Beauty World DT5.

Based on transaction data in the last 12 months, sale prices range from S$ 1,573 psf to S$ 1,995 psf at an average of S$ 1,841 psf. Its highest recorded transaction was S$ 2,056 psf in Nov 2022 for a 1,012-sqft unit. Its lowest recorded transaction was S$ 554 psf in Aug 2005 for a 1,119-sqft unit. Its current rental yield is at 2.5%. Other nearby amenities include: 3 Grocerie(s): Fairprice Finest, Cold Storage, Koryo Mart Bukit Timah, and 3 Shopping Mall(s): KAP Mall, Bukit Timah Plaza, Beauty World Plaza.

Buyers and tenants are attracted to District 21, which comprises of Upper Bukit Timah, Ulu Pandan and Clementi Park. Homes at these areas are generally nestled in the green enclave of Bukit Timah Nature Reserves and Bukit Batok Town Park. District 21 is served by Clementi MRT station of the East-West Line, as well as Beauty World, Hillview, Cashew and Bukit Panjang MRT stations of the Downtown Line. Accessibility to other parts of Singapore is also enhanced due to its close proximity to the Pan-Island Expressway. As one of the oldest HDB towns in Singapore, Clementi has grown into a vibrant town with many tertiary educational institutions, such as the National University of Singapore. Clementi Town centre, a mixed-use development in the heart of Clementi, comprises of a shopping mall, an air-conditioned bus interchange, a public library and two 40-storey residential buildings. Other major amenities here include the 12ha Clementi Woods Park, the Clementi Sports Hall and Clementi Swimming Complex.

As a major financial and business hub, the Central Region stands as the dynamic city centre of Singapore. Spanning 13,150ha in land area, the region hosts Singapore's only UNESCO World Heritage Site and is home to many of the country's national monuments, such as the Merlion Park and Statue of Raffles. Although the region is designated mainly for commercial activities, it houses residential developments ranging from HDBs to bungalows. There is also plenty of greenery in the region, linked by 19km of park connectors. According to the Master Plan 2019, Orchard and Downtown will be rejuvenated as a lifestyle hub to include more mixed developments which offer live, work and play opportunities. New housing will be constructed in the Marina South Area with community facilities and amenities. Stage-6 of the Circle Line opening by 2025 will also enhance the Central Region's connectivity.
