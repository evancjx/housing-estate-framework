How this planner calculates the result

Return definitions

projected sale = purchase price × (1 + annual growth)holding years

economic profit = sale − purchase − acquisition costs − sale costs − SSD − mortgage interest − holding costs + net rent

Mortgage principal is not subtracted again: it either reduces the loan balance or returns as owner equity.

Ledger reconciliation

row difference = payment amount − cash − CPF − bank loan

Property-payment rows reconcile to the purchase price. Acquisition costs reconcile separately, while timeline notes have no financial value.

Duties included

  • BSD is estimated automatically on the higher entered purchase price or market value.
  • ABSD is included only when you enter an amount.
  • Mortgage duty is estimated at 0.4% of the loan, capped at S$500.
  • SSD is estimated from exact entered legal dates and the higher entered sale value.

Planning limits

  • The ledger is not evidence of beneficial ownership or debt liability.
  • CPF accrued interest is excluded unless entered in the refund field.
  • Developer-specific clauses, bank fees and exact lender accrual are not inferred.
  • Confirm every amount and payment date against signed documents.

This is an educational scenario tool, not a valuation, forecast, offer of credit, tax opinion, ownership declaration or conveyancing advice. Confirm contractual payments, loan liability, CPF usage and refund, stamp duties, legal dates and exemptions with the relevant bank, developer, CPF Board, IRAS and your solicitor.
